# Captain's Log 2192.148 — Glass Tide Surge


Recorded during the midwatch; some words are lost to static. The harvest came in thin but alive. Every leaf was counted twice, and root rot crisis was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Glass Tide Surge aftermath. The notes on Glass Tide Surge witness account run to several pages. The notes on algae ration run to several pages. The survey team spent a week on root rot crisis alone.

**Why the glass tide surge was retired.** The harvest came in thin but alive; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

Further reading: *Notes on Closed-Loop Water at Scale* (Quill, 2182).

Further reading: *On The Salt Blight Post-Mortem* (Brightwater, 2188).
