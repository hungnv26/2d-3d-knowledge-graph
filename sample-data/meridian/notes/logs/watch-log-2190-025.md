# Watch Log 2190.025


This entry was written in a hurry. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and the Halvorsen serum was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Watch Log 2190.025 timeline alone. Nobody agreed on what Watch Log 2190.025 aftermath really meant. The crew learned about contagion modelling the hard way. Field surgery came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged phantom symptoms before anyone else noticed it.

See also [[survey-of-orzoran-station|Survey of Orzoran Station]]. The same pattern showed up at [[plasma-compass|Plasma compass]]. This connects to [[watch-log-2194-028|Watch Log 2194.028]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[orrery|ORRERY]].
