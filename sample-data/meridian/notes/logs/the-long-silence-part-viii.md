# The Long Silence, Part VIII


The following is transcribed from the ship’s voice recorder. Laughter carried further than it should in the low gravity. Someone had strung lights along the corridor again, and the captain’s table was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Long Silence aftermath run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what Long Silence timeline really meant. Nobody agreed on what the captain’s table really meant.

Letters from home were read aloud, twice. The silence outside the hull had started to feel like a presence.

Someone had strung lights along the corridor again. The midwatch supper ran long and nobody minded.
