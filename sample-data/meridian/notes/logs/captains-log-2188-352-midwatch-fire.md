# Captain's Log 2188.352 — Midwatch Fire


The following is transcribed from the ship’s voice recorder. The council drifted apart and together like cloud. The treaty was written in weather, not ink, and Vell migration law was never far from anyone's mind.

Midwatch Fire aftermath came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Midwatch Fire alarm. Most of the arguments aboard came back to drift-speech. The crew learned about wind-reading the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the returned gift.

[[ormors-bell-quarantine|Ormor's Bell Quarantine]] tells the other side of the story. This connects to [[frozen-shoals|Frozen Shoals]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[frozen-kelp|Frozen Kelp]]. See also [[survey-of-velvet-belt-of-harzormor|Survey of Velvet Belt of Harzormor]].

A gift was returned, which meant everything. The treaty was written in weather, not ink.
