# Captain's Log 2192.226 — Missing Hour


Recorded during the midwatch; some words are lost to static. Someone had strung lights along the corridor again. Letters from home were read aloud, twice, and homesick hour was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Missing Hour witness account before anyone else noticed it. The Missing Hour alarm came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Missing Hour aftermath really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the midwatch supper.

Compare the account in [[captain-tallulah-rasmussen|Captain Tallulah Rasmussen]]. [[watch-log-2191-147|Watch Log 2191.147]] tells the other side of the story.
