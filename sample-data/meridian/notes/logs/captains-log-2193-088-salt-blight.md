# Captain's Log 2193.088 — Salt Blight


Recorded during the midwatch; some words are lost to static. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest, and field fabrication was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Salt Blight witness account the hard way. The survey team spent a week on the Salt Blight alarm alone. ORRERY flagged the cannibal refit before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what salvage rights really meant. Improvised shielding came up in every debrief afterwards.
