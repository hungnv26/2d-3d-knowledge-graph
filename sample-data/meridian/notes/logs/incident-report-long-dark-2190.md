# Incident Report: Long Dark (2190)


Recorded during the midwatch; some words are lost to static. The council drifted apart and together like cloud. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and the returned gift was never far from anyone's mind.

The Long Dark alarm is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what Long Dark timeline really meant. ORRERY flagged Long Dark aftermath before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about Long Dark witness account the hard way. Kinship by weather is still an open question in the archive. The gift exchange came up in every debrief afterwards.
