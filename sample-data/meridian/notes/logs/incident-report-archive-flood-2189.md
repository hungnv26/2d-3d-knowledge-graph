# Incident Report: Archive Flood (2189)


The following is transcribed from the ship’s voice recorder. The midwatch supper ran long and nobody minded. Someone had strung lights along the corridor again, and homesick hour was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Archive Flood witness account before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged Archive Flood aftermath before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the letter archive. Nobody agreed on what first-snow tradition really meant.

Crew who worked on [[survey-of-uldarmel-vi|Survey of Uldarmel VI]] disagreed. This connects to [[survey-of-xanras-veil|Survey of Xanra's Veil]] in ways that are still being argued over. The same pattern showed up at [[lieutenant-kofi-rasmussen|Lieutenant Kofi Rasmussen]]. See also [[watch-log-2192-210|Watch Log 2192.210]]. Compare the account in [[incident-report-vell-gift-2191|Incident Report: Vell Gift (2191)]]. Crew who worked on [[watch-log-2191-147|Watch Log 2191.147]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[captains-log-2187-087-glass-tide-surge|Captain's Log 2187.087 — Glass Tide Surge]] disagreed.

**Why the archive flood must idle before a jump.** The midwatch supper ran long and nobody minded; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
