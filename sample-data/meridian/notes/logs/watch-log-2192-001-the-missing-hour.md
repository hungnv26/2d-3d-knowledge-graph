# Watch Log 2192.001 — The Missing Hour


This entry was written in a hurry. The crew spoke in whispers without deciding to. No signal came back, not even static, and unspoken orbit was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what the The Missing Hour alarm really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to The Missing Hour witness account. Most of the arguments aboard came back to dead-air geometry. Signal absence is still an open question in the archive.

This connects to [[stillwater-deep|Stillwater Deep]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[operation-whisperfield-recovery|Operation Whisperfield Recovery]]. [[the-silent-cluster|The Silent Cluster]] tells the other side of the story.

**Why the the missing hour is kept behind two doors.** The instruments reported nothing, perfectly; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

The instruments reported nothing, perfectly. An hour was missing from every clock aboard.
