# Captain's Log 2191.323 — Lantern Signal


Recorded during the midwatch; some words are lost to static. The serum held, barely. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and spore quarantine was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Lantern Signal aftermath the hard way. The survey team spent a week on the Lantern Signal alarm alone. Nobody agreed on what the isolation ward really meant. ORRERY flagged long-sleep recovery before anyone else noticed it. The immunity map is still an open question in the archive.

See also [[hull-anchor|Hull anchor]]. Compare the account in [[watch-log-2194-028|Watch Log 2194.028]].

**Why the lantern signal is kept behind two doors.** The fever broke on the ninth day; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

Further reading: *On The Blue Fever Outbreak* (Delacroix, 2181).
