# The Long Silence, Part III


Recorded during the midwatch; some words are lost to static. Someone had strung lights along the corridor again. The midwatch supper ran long and nobody minded, and birthday protocol was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about the Long Silence alarm the hard way. Nobody agreed on what Long Silence timeline really meant. ORRERY flagged Long Silence aftermath before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to deck-four choir. First-snow tradition is still an open question in the archive.

Compare the account in [[the-long-silence-part-iv|The Long Silence, Part IV]].

Letters from home were read aloud, twice. The silence outside the hull had started to feel like a presence.

**Why the long silence is kept behind two doors.** The midwatch supper ran long and nobody minded; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
