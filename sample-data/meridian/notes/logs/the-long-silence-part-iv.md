# The Long Silence, Part IV


Recorded during the midwatch; some words are lost to static. Letters from home were read aloud, twice. Laughter carried further than it should in the low gravity, and watch rotation was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Long Silence timeline alone. The crew learned about Long Silence witness account the hard way. Nobody agreed on what Long Silence aftermath really meant. The crew learned about long-haul fatigue the hard way.
