# Incident Report: Drift Storm (2193)


Recorded during the midwatch; some words are lost to static. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and spore quarantine was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Drift Storm witness account really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Drift Storm timeline. Nobody agreed on what the Drift Storm alarm really meant. Nobody agreed on what the isolation ward really meant.
