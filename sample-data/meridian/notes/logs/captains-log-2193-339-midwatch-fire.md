# Captain's Log 2193.339 — Midwatch Fire


Recorded during the midwatch; some words are lost to static. The green deck smelled of rain that never fell. Every leaf was counted twice, and mycelium bricks was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Midwatch Fire witness account the hard way. The Midwatch Fire alarm came up in every debrief afterwards. Midwatch Fire timeline came up in every debrief afterwards. Midwatch Fire aftermath is still an open question in the archive. The notes on algae ration run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what emergency sprouting really meant.
