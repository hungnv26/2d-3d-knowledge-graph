# Watch Log 2194.028


This entry was written in a hurry. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. The serum held, barely, and contagion modelling was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Watch Log 2194.028 witness account alone. ORRERY flagged the Watch Log 2194.028 alarm before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to contagion modelling.

Crew who worked on [[hull-scrubber|Hull scrubber]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[ember-eels|Ember Eels]]. The same pattern showed up at [[drowned-wardens|Drowned Wardens]]. This connects to [[venciny-moss|Venciny Moss]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[plasma-shield|Plasma shield]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[silent-vaults-of-cimel-quarantine|Silent Vaults of Cimel Quarantine]]. See also [[orrery|ORRERY]].

**Why the watch log 2194.028 is kept behind two doors.** Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
