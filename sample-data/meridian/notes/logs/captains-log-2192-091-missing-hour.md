# Captain's Log 2192.091 — Missing Hour


This entry was written in a hurry. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and the isolation ward was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on the Missing Hour alarm alone. The crew learned about Missing Hour aftermath the hard way. Nobody agreed on what Missing Hour witness account really meant. The crew learned about the sickbay ledger the hard way.
