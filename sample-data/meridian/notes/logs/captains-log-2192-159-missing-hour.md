# Captain's Log 2192.159 — Missing Hour


This entry was written in a hurry. Every leaf was counted twice. The green deck smelled of rain that never fell, and rationing ladder was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Missing Hour timeline really meant. Missing Hour aftermath came up in every debrief afterwards. The Missing Hour alarm came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about nutrient cascade the hard way.

Crew who worked on [[plasma-crystal|Plasma crystal]] disagreed. This connects to [[operation-driftwood|Operation Driftwood]] in ways that are still being argued over.
