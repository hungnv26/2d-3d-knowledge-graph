# The Vell Accords


An index, kept by ORRERY and corrected by hand. The treaty was written in weather, not ink. The council drifted apart and together like cloud, and trade in silence was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what the returned gift really meant. The survey team spent a week on the Vell alone. Vell honour debts came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the gift exchange. ORRERY flagged trade in silence before anyone else noticed it.
