# Field Guide to the Deeps


An index, kept by ORRERY and corrected by hand. Samples sang faintly in their jars. The water glowed a patient blue, and shoal mind was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about spore-song the hard way. The survey team spent a week on lantern chemistry alone. ORRERY flagged bioluminescent grammar before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to hydrothermal gardens. The survey team spent a week on abyssal chorus alone.
