# Hazard Register


An index, kept by ORRERY and corrected by hand. Space folded like wet paper. The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance, and jump echo was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on harmonic lock run to several pages. The crew learned about the Adeyemi limit the hard way. The quiet jump is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to resonance drift. The survey team spent a week on exotic matter ballast alone.
