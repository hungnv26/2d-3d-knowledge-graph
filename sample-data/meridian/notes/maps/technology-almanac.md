# Technology Almanac


An index, kept by ORRERY and corrected by hand. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint, and the engineer’s oath was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what the junkyard fleet really meant. Reactor patchwork is still an open question in the archive. The cannibal refit is still an open question in the archive. Derelict etiquette came up in every debrief afterwards. Hull mending came up in every debrief afterwards.
