# Captain’s Collected Logs


An index, kept by ORRERY and corrected by hand. Someone had strung lights along the corridor again. Letters from home were read aloud, twice, and first-snow tradition was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on the chess ladder alone. Nobody agreed on what birthday protocol really meant. Nobody agreed on what watch rotation really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to grief rotation. ORRERY flagged shore-leave lottery before anyone else noticed it.
