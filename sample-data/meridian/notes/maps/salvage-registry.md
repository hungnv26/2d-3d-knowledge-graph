# Salvage Registry


This page gathers everything the archive holds on its subject. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail. They built what they needed out of what they found, and stress fractures was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged the junkyard fleet before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on hull mending alone. Structural fatigue is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the engineer’s oath. The crew learned about the cannibal refit the hard way.
