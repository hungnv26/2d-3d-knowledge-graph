# Bestiary of Oru


This page gathers everything the archive holds on its subject. Samples sang faintly in their jars. The tide came in as glass and went out as song, and current-walking was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to the glass tide. The drowning light came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on tidal symbiosis alone. Abyssal chorus came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged hydrothermal gardens before anyone else noticed it.
