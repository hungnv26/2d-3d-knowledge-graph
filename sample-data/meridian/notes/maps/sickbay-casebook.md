# Sickbay Casebook


This page gathers everything the archive holds on its subject. The fever broke on the ninth day. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and xeno-allergens was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged the immunity map before anyone else noticed it. Xeno-allergens is still an open question in the archive. Cryo triage came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the blue fever. Nobody agreed on what phantom symptoms really meant.
