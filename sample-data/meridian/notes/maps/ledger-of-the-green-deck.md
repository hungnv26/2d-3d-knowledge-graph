# Ledger of the Green Deck


This page gathers everything the archive holds on its subject. Water was the only currency that mattered. The harvest came in thin but alive, and the long harvest was never far from anyone's mind.

Compost engine is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged the green deck before anyone else noticed it. Light starvation came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about algae ration the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the long harvest.
