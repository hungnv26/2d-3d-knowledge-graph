# Atlas of the Outer Reach


This page gathers everything the archive holds on its subject. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The survey drone came back with more questions, and parallax ladders was never far from anyone's mind.

Dust rivers is still an open question in the archive. The crew learned about lensed horizons the hard way. The survey team spent a week on waypoint decay alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the edge of the map. Nobody agreed on what gravity shoals really meant.
