# Rescue at Thalselthal XI


Rescue at Thalselthal XI was logged as lost in 2190. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. Every chamber faced the same distant point, and the sealed vault was never far from anyone's mind.

Thalselthal XI landing party came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Thalselthal XI objective before anyone else noticed it. The notes on Thalselthal XI casualty report run to several pages. The crew learned about Thalselthal XI debrief the hard way. The third inscription is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged the sealed vault before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about the drowned library the hard way.

[[ululdars-hearth|Ululdar's Hearth]] tells the other side of the story. Crew who worked on [[ciithul-ix|Ciithul IX]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[survey-of-xanras-veil|Survey of Xanra's Veil]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[captain-ingrid-achterberg|Captain Ingrid Achterberg]] disagreed.

The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose.
