# Survey of Ventethny II


The board marked Survey of Ventethny II planned in 2189, which only tells half of it. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The survey drone came back with more questions, and the Outer Reach was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Ventethny II supply manifest run to several pages. Ventethny II objective came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Ventethny II casualty report really meant. The crew learned about the cartographer’s error the hard way. Parallax ladders is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on uncharted silence alone.
