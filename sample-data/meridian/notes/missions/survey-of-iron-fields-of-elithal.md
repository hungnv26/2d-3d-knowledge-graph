# Survey of Iron Fields of Elithal


The board marked Survey of Iron Fields of Elithal lost in 2192, which only tells half of it. The charts ended and the dark began. The map was redrawn for the ninth time, and the dark meridian was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Iron Fields of Elithal debrief before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on Iron Fields of Elithal supply manifest alone. The notes on drift charts run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the dark meridian.
