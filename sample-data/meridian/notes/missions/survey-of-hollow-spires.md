# Survey of Hollow Spires


The board marked Survey of Hollow Spires succeeded in 2189, which only tells half of it. Nobody spoke first, by design. The protocol said wait, and they waited, and gesture grammar was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Hollow Spires supply manifest before anyone else noticed it. Hollow Spires casualty report is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what observer drift really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the silent greeting. The survey team spent a week on translation lattice alone.

[[iron-shelf|Iron Shelf]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[operation-ninth-bell-iii|Operation Ninth Bell III]]. Crew who worked on [[amber-swarm|Amber Swarm]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[uldarmel-vi|Uldarmel VI]] disagreed. See also [[dr-aurelio-adeyemi|Dr. Aurelio Adeyemi]].
