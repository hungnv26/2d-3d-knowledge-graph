# Survey of Uldarmel VI


The board marked Survey of Uldarmel VI succeeded in 2190, which only tells half of it. The treaty was written in weather, not ink. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and wind-reading was never far from anyone's mind.

Uldarmel VI objective came up in every debrief afterwards. Uldarmel VI landing party came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the listening embassy.

The same pattern showed up at [[frozen-shoals|Frozen Shoals]]. This connects to [[plasma-shield|Plasma shield]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[incident-report-long-dark-2190|Incident Report: Long Dark (2190)]]. Compare the account in [[kaqus-mirror|Kaqu's Mirror]]. [[pale-gardens|Pale Gardens]] tells the other side of the story. See also [[ensign-kenji-moreau|Ensign Kenji Moreau]].

**Why the uldarmel vi crew rotates every watch.** The treaty was written in weather, not ink; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

A gift was returned, which meant everything. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed.
