# Operation Keystone


Operation Keystone was logged as active in 2193. The survey drone came back with more questions. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and gravity shoals was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Keystone supply manifest really meant. Nobody agreed on what Keystone casualty report really meant. The survey team spent a week on Keystone landing party alone. Nobody agreed on what Keystone objective really meant. Nobody agreed on what the cartographer’s error really meant. The crew learned about parallax ladders the hard way.

The same pattern showed up at [[silent-leviathans|Silent Leviathans]]. This connects to [[lattice-cradle|Lattice cradle]] in ways that are still being argued over. The same pattern showed up at [[orrery|ORRERY]]. Crew who worked on [[commander-luca-ekwueme|Commander Luca Ekwueme]] disagreed.
