# Operation Emberline VII


Operation Emberline VII began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The fever broke on the ninth day. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and contagion modelling was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Emberline VII debrief. The survey team spent a week on Emberline VII objective alone. Emberline VII landing party is still an open question in the archive. The notes on cryo triage run to several pages. The crew learned about the isolation ward the hard way. The crew learned about phantom symptoms the hard way.
