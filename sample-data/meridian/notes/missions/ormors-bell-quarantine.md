# Ormor's Bell Quarantine


Ormor's Bell Quarantine began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. A gift was returned, which meant everything. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and Vell honour debts was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Ormor's Bell supply manifest alone. Nobody agreed on what Ormor's Bell objective really meant. The notes on wind-reading run to several pages. Kinship by weather came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Vell honour debts before anyone else noticed it.
