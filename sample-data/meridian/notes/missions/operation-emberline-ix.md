# Operation Emberline IX


The board marked Operation Emberline IX lost in 2188, which only tells half of it. The council drifted apart and together like cloud. A gift was returned, which meant everything, and the Vell was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Emberline IX casualty report really meant. Emberline IX objective came up in every debrief afterwards. Emberline IX supply manifest is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Emberline IX landing party. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vell honour debts. Most of the arguments aboard came back to shared-sky accord.

[[dundarushs-cradle|Dundarush's Cradle]] tells the other side of the story. Compare the account in [[survey-of-anushkir-station|Survey of Anushkir Station]].

**Why the emberline ix is kept behind two doors.** The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
