# Operation Seedvault


Operation Seedvault began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The fever broke on the ninth day. The serum held, barely, and the sickbay ledger was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Seedvault landing party the hard way. Nobody agreed on what Seedvault casualty report really meant. Cryo triage is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged long-sleep recovery before anyone else noticed it. The blue fever is still an open question in the archive.

[[survey-of-orzoran-station|Survey of Orzoran Station]] tells the other side of the story. Compare the account in [[rescue-at-nyriss-cradle|Rescue at Nyris's Cradle]]. [[glass-shoals|Glass Shoals]] tells the other side of the story.

**Why the seedvault crew rotates every watch.** The fever broke on the ninth day; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
