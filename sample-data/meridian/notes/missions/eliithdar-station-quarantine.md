# Eliithdar Station Quarantine


Eliithdar Station Quarantine began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The fever broke on the ninth day. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and the Halvorsen serum was never far from anyone's mind.

Eliithdar Station landing party is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on Eliithdar Station casualty report alone. Nobody agreed on what Eliithdar Station debrief really meant. ORRERY flagged Eliithdar Station objective before anyone else noticed it. The isolation ward is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to cryo triage. The crew learned about xeno-allergens the hard way.

**Why the eliithdar station is never run unattended.** Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

The serum held, barely. The fever broke on the ninth day.
