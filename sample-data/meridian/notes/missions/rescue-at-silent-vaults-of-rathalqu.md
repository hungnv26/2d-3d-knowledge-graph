# Rescue at Silent Vaults of Rathalqu


Rescue at Silent Vaults of Rathalqu began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four. The survey drone came back with more questions, and drift charts was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Silent Vaults of Rathalqu debrief before anyone else noticed it. Silent Vaults of Rathalqu casualty report came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Silent Vaults of Rathalqu landing party before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on Silent Vaults of Rathalqu supply manifest alone. ORRERY flagged parallax ladders before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged orbital census before anyone else noticed it. Gravity shoals came up in every debrief afterwards.

Crew who worked on [[cryo-anchor|Cryo anchor]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[hungry-deeps|Hungry Deeps]] disagreed. See also [[ensign-arjun-rasmussen|Ensign Arjun Rasmussen]].

Three beacons flickered where there should have been four. The map was redrawn for the ninth time.
