# Xananeli Station Quarantine


Xananeli Station Quarantine began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and the long survey was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Xananeli Station landing party really meant. Nobody agreed on what Xananeli Station casualty report really meant. Beacon chains came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about drift charts the hard way.

This connects to [[cians-orchard|Cian's Orchard]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[thalbovess-needle|Thalboves's Needle]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[echo-anchor|Echo anchor]] disagreed. [[the-velvet-fields-of-boaeven|The Velvet Fields of Boaeven]] tells the other side of the story. Crew who worked on [[burning-leviathans|Burning Leviathans]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[silent-loom|Silent loom]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[orrery|ORRERY]].

**Why the xananeli station was retired.** Three beacons flickered where there should have been four; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

Further reading: *On Gravity Shoals of the Outer Reach* (Oyelaran, 2188).

The charts ended and the dark began. The survey drone came back with more questions.
