# Cikirush XI Quarantine


Cikirush XI Quarantine was logged as succeeded in 2187. The survey drone came back with more questions. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and lensed horizons was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Cikirush XI objective before anyone else noticed it. Cikirush XI debrief came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Cikirush XI supply manifest really meant. Nobody agreed on what uncharted silence really meant.
