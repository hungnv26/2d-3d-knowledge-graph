# Operation Ninth Bell


Operation Ninth Bell began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest, and salvage rights was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ninth Bell supply manifest. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ninth Bell debrief. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ninth Bell landing party. The spare-parts ledger is still an open question in the archive.

See also [[watch-log-2188-238|Watch Log 2188.238]]. The same pattern showed up at [[singing-spires|Singing Spires]]. Compare the account in [[spore-shield|Spore shield]]. [[incident-report-glass-tide-surge-2190|Incident Report: Glass Tide Surge (2190)]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[rescue-at-singing-spires|Rescue at Singing Spires]].

**Why the ninth bell protocol waits eleven minutes.** They built what they needed out of what they found; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint.
