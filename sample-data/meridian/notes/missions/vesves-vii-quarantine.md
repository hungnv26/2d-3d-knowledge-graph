# Vesves VII Quarantine


The board marked Vesves VII Quarantine succeeded in 2189, which only tells half of it. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed. The council drifted apart and together like cloud, and the drifter council was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Vesves VII landing party run to several pages. Vesves VII debrief came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on Vesves VII casualty report alone. Nobody agreed on what Vesves VII supply manifest really meant. The survey team spent a week on trade in silence alone. ORRERY flagged the gift exchange before anyone else noticed it.
