# Vesny IX Quarantine


Vesny IX Quarantine was logged as failed in 2188. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The survey drone came back with more questions, and lensed horizons was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vesny IX casualty report. The crew learned about Vesny IX landing party the hard way. Vesny IX debrief is still an open question in the archive. Star-fall is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged the long survey before anyone else noticed it.
