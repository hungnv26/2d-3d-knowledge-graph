# Ciithul IX Quarantine


Ciithul IX Quarantine began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them, and buried observatories was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Ciithul IX debrief run to several pages. The crew learned about Ciithul IX objective the hard way. The survey team spent a week on Ciithul IX casualty report alone. Nobody agreed on what star-map murals really meant. ORRERY flagged ossuary gardens before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged the drowned library before anyone else noticed it.
