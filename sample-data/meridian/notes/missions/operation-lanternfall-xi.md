# Operation Lanternfall XI


Operation Lanternfall XI was logged as planned in 2187. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The survey drone came back with more questions, and the dark meridian was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Lanternfall XI casualty report before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Lanternfall XI debrief. The notes on Lanternfall XI objective run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what Lanternfall XI supply manifest really meant. The survey team spent a week on the dark meridian alone. Nobody agreed on what uncharted silence really meant. Nobody agreed on what waypoint decay really meant.

**Why the lanternfall xi protocol waits eleven minutes.** The survey drone came back with more questions; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
