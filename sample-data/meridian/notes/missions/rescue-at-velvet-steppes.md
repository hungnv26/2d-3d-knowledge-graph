# Rescue at Velvet Steppes


Rescue at Velvet Steppes began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make. Every chamber faced the same distant point, and spiral glyphs was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Velvet Steppes debrief alone. Velvet Steppes objective came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Velvet Steppes casualty report. The survey team spent a week on the absent builders alone. The survey team spent a week on the third inscription alone.

[[star-lance|Star lance]] tells the other side of the story. Crew who worked on [[thalselthal-xi|Thalselthal XI]] disagreed. See also [[rescue-at-nyriss-cradle|Rescue at Nyris's Cradle]]. This connects to [[the-singing-deeps-of-aesel|The Singing Deeps of Aesel]] in ways that are still being argued over. The same pattern showed up at [[dr-elif-solberg|Dr. Elif Solberg]].

The builders had left, carefully, on purpose. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them.
