# Operation Lanternfall IV


Operation Lanternfall IV began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The survey drone came back with more questions. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and beacon chains was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Lanternfall IV landing party before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Lanternfall IV supply manifest. Lanternfall IV objective came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on orbital census run to several pages. The crew learned about drift charts the hard way.

Crew who worked on [[rescue-at-silent-vaults-of-rathalqu|Rescue at Silent Vaults of Rathalqu]] disagreed. This connects to [[operation-keystone-xi|Operation Keystone XI]] in ways that are still being argued over. This connects to [[keplers-lantern|Kepler's Lantern]] in ways that are still being argued over. The same pattern showed up at [[operation-keystone|Operation Keystone]]. This connects to [[raovven-station|Raovven Station]] in ways that are still being argued over.

The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The charts ended and the dark began.
