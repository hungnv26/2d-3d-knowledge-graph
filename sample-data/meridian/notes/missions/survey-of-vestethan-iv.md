# Survey of Vestethan IV


Survey of Vestethan IV was logged as lost in 2187. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. Every chamber faced the same distant point, and the drowned library was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vestethan IV casualty report. Vestethan IV objective came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what the Precursors really meant. Chronolith is still an open question in the archive. The notes on the drowned library run to several pages.

This connects to [[boithcis-lantern|Boithci's Lantern]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[iron-fields-of-elithal-quarantine|Iron Fields of Elithal Quarantine]]. Compare the account in [[navigator-ingrid-solberg|Navigator Ingrid Solberg]].

**Why the vestethan iv is kept behind two doors.** The glyphs were older than the star that lit them; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

Every chamber faced the same distant point. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make.
