# Hungry Deeps Quarantine


Hungry Deeps Quarantine began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. A gift was returned, which meant everything. The treaty was written in weather, not ink, and kinship by weather was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Hungry Deeps objective alone. Hungry Deeps casualty report is still an open question in the archive. The crew learned about the drifter council the hard way. Trade in silence is still an open question in the archive. Hospitality rites came up in every debrief afterwards.
