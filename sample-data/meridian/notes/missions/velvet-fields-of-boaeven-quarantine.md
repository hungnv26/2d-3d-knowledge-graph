# Velvet Fields of Boaeven Quarantine


Velvet Fields of Boaeven Quarantine began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest, and the engineer’s oath was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Velvet Fields of Boaeven supply manifest the hard way. The notes on Velvet Fields of Boaeven casualty report run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to zero-g welding. Field fabrication is still an open question in the archive.
