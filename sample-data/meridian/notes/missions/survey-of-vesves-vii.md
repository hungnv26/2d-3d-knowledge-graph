# Survey of Vesves VII


Survey of Vesves VII began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. They built what they needed out of what they found, and hull mending was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vesves VII objective. Vesves VII landing party came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Vesves VII supply manifest really meant. The notes on the junkyard fleet run to several pages. Hull mending is still an open question in the archive.

Compare the account in [[tidal-abacus|Tidal abacus]]. See also [[captain-amara-zhao|Captain Amara Zhao]].

The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest.
