# Operation Ironveil


Operation Ironveil was logged as active in 2192. The charts ended and the dark began. The map was redrawn for the ninth time, and waypoint decay was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Ironveil casualty report run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ironveil landing party. The crew learned about dust rivers the hard way. ORRERY flagged the dark meridian before anyone else noticed it.

The survey drone came back with more questions. The charts ended and the dark began.
