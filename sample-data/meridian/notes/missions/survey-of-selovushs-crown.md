# Survey of Selovush's Crown


Survey of Selovush's Crown began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The survey drone came back with more questions. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and the dark meridian was never far from anyone's mind.

Selovush's Crown supply manifest is still an open question in the archive. The crew learned about Selovush's Crown objective the hard way. ORRERY flagged the dark meridian before anyone else noticed it. Waypoint decay came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on lensed horizons run to several pages.

The same pattern showed up at [[ululmor-station|Ululmor Station]]. Compare the account in [[memory-scrubber|Memory scrubber]]. This connects to [[darkas-needle|Darka's Needle]] in ways that are still being argued over. The same pattern showed up at [[vesny-ix-quarantine|Vesny IX Quarantine]]. [[commander-mei-moreau|Commander Mei Moreau]] tells the other side of the story.

The charts ended and the dark began. The map was redrawn for the ninth time.
