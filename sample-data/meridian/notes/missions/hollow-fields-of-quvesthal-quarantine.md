# Hollow Fields of Quvesthal Quarantine


Hollow Fields of Quvesthal Quarantine began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The council drifted apart and together like cloud. The treaty was written in weather, not ink, and trade in silence was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Hollow Fields of Quvesthal landing party run to several pages. Hollow Fields of Quvesthal objective came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about the Vell the hard way. The listening embassy came up in every debrief afterwards.
