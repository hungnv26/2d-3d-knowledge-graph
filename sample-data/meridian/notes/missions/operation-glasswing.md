# Operation Glasswing


The board marked Operation Glasswing lost in 2191, which only tells half of it. Samples sang faintly in their jars. Something below the shelf answered in light, and abyssal chorus was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Glasswing landing party. Glasswing debrief is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what Glasswing casualty report really meant. Glasswing objective is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on the drowning light alone.

[[harov-station|Harov Station]] tells the other side of the story. This connects to [[crimson-shoals|Crimson Shoals]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[chief-bao-petrakis|Chief Bao Petrakis]] disagreed. See also [[hull-anchor|Hull anchor]]. The same pattern showed up at [[the-silent-vaults-of-cimel|The Silent Vaults of Cimel]].
