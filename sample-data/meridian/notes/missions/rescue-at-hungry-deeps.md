# Rescue at Hungry Deeps


Rescue at Hungry Deeps began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed. The treaty was written in weather, not ink, and shared-sky accord was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Hungry Deeps objective before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about Hungry Deeps debrief the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Hungry Deeps landing party. Nobody agreed on what Hungry Deeps supply manifest really meant. ORRERY flagged kinship by weather before anyone else noticed it.

[[iron-kelp|Iron Kelp]] tells the other side of the story. Compare the account in [[navigator-bao-achterberg|Navigator Bao Achterberg]]. Crew who worked on [[drowned-rays|Drowned Rays]] disagreed. This connects to [[rescue-at-kaqus-mirror|Rescue at Kaqu's Mirror]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[ormors-bell-quarantine|Ormor's Bell Quarantine]].

**Why the hungry deeps protocol waits eleven minutes.** A gift was returned, which meant everything; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
