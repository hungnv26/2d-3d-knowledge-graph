# Survey of Hollow Steppes


Survey of Hollow Steppes began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose, and the absent builders was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Hollow Steppes debrief the hard way. Hollow Steppes landing party is still an open question in the archive. The notes on Hollow Steppes objective run to several pages. ORRERY flagged Hollow Steppes supply manifest before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on the sealed vault alone.

Crew who worked on [[ciithul-ix|Ciithul IX]] disagreed. [[rescue-at-selharny-station|Rescue at Selharny Station]] tells the other side of the story.

**Why the hollow steppes is kept behind two doors.** The builders had left, carefully, on purpose; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
