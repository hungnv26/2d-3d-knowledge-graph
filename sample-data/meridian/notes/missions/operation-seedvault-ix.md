# Operation Seedvault IX


The board marked Operation Seedvault IX active in 2191, which only tells half of it. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose, and spiral glyphs was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Seedvault IX landing party alone. Nobody agreed on what Seedvault IX objective really meant. Seedvault IX debrief came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Seedvault IX casualty report before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on harmonic masonry alone. The survey team spent a week on the patient architecture alone. The survey team spent a week on the absent builders alone.
