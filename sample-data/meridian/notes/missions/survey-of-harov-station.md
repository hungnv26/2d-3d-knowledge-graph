# Survey of Harov Station


Survey of Harov Station was logged as active in 2187. The water glowed a patient blue. Samples sang faintly in their jars, and the pale migration was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Harov Station landing party alone. The notes on Harov Station casualty report run to several pages. The crew learned about Harov Station supply manifest the hard way. The drowning light is still an open question in the archive. The pale migration came up in every debrief afterwards.

Crew who worked on [[frozen-kelp|Frozen Kelp]] disagreed. Compare the account in [[rescue-at-kirulith-ix|Rescue at Kirulith IX]]. Crew who worked on [[harov-station|Harov Station]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[dr-ravi-umarov|Dr. Ravi Umarov]] disagreed.
