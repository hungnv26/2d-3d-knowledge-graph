# Rescue at Silent Vaults of Cimel


The board marked Rescue at Silent Vaults of Cimel planned in 2187, which only tells half of it. The room held its breath for eleven minutes. The protocol said wait, and they waited, and contact quarantine was never far from anyone's mind.
