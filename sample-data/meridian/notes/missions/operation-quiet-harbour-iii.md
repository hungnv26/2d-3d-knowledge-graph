# Operation Quiet Harbour III


Operation Quiet Harbour III began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The protocol said wait, and they waited. Nobody spoke first, by design, and mirror protocol was never far from anyone's mind.

Quiet Harbour III supply manifest is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on Quiet Harbour III debrief alone.
