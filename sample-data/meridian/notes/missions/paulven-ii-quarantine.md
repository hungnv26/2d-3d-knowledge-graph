# Paulven II Quarantine


The board marked Paulven II Quarantine active in 2189, which only tells half of it. Nobody spoke first, by design. A single prime number blinked back, and contact quarantine was never far from anyone's mind.

Paulven II casualty report came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on Paulven II supply manifest alone. Paulven II debrief is still an open question in the archive. The notes on Paulven II objective run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to reciprocal pause.

The same pattern showed up at [[amber-swarm|Amber Swarm]]. See also [[captain-kofi-solberg|Captain Kofi Solberg]]. Crew who worked on [[captain-oskar-castellanos|Captain Oskar Castellanos]] disagreed. See also [[dunxans-harbour|Dunxan's Harbour]]. The same pattern showed up at [[the-singing-reach-of-dundunhar|The Singing Reach of Dundunhar]]. Compare the account in [[the-hollow-fields-of-quvesthal|The Hollow Fields of Quvesthal]]. Crew who worked on [[orrery|ORRERY]] disagreed. See also [[specialist-luca-achterberg|Specialist Luca Achterberg]].
