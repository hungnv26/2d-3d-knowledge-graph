# Rescue at Kepler's Lantern


Rescue at Kepler's Lantern was logged as succeeded in 2192. They built what they needed out of what they found. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail, and salvage rights was never far from anyone's mind.

Kepler's Lantern casualty report came up in every debrief afterwards. Kepler's Lantern objective is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on improvised shielding alone.
