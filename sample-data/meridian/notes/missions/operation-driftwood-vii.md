# Operation Driftwood VII


Operation Driftwood VII was logged as active in 2192. A gift was returned, which meant everything. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and kinship by weather was never far from anyone's mind.

Driftwood VII objective is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Driftwood VII casualty report. Nobody agreed on what Driftwood VII debrief really meant. Drift-speech came up in every debrief afterwards. Vell honour debts came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged the Vell before anyone else noticed it.

[[velvet-crawlers|Velvet Crawlers]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[captains-log-2188-352-midwatch-fire|Captain's Log 2188.352 — Midwatch Fire]]. This connects to [[burning-moss|Burning Moss]] in ways that are still being argued over. The same pattern showed up at [[operation-glasswing-xi|Operation Glasswing XI]]. This connects to [[incident-report-long-dark-2190|Incident Report: Long Dark (2190)]] in ways that are still being argued over.
