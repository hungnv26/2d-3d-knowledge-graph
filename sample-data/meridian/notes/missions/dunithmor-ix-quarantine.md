# Dunithmor IX Quarantine


Dunithmor IX Quarantine was logged as failed in 2190. Nobody spoke first, by design. The protocol said wait, and they waited, and reciprocal pause was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Dunithmor IX landing party really meant. The notes on Dunithmor IX casualty report run to several pages. The crew learned about Dunithmor IX objective the hard way. The survey team spent a week on intent signalling alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to mirror protocol.

Crew who worked on [[selovushs-crown|Selovush's Crown]] disagreed. See also [[silent-reach|Silent Reach]]. The same pattern showed up at [[hungry-deeps|Hungry Deeps]]. [[rispaor-vii|Rispaor VII]] tells the other side of the story. [[rescue-at-rara-v|Rescue at Rara V]] tells the other side of the story. This connects to [[dr-mei-castellanos|Dr. Mei Castellanos]] in ways that are still being argued over.

Further reading: *On The Mirror Protocol* (Takeda, 2192).
