# Operation Seedvault VII


Operation Seedvault VII was logged as active in 2188. Every chamber faced the same distant point. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose, and chronolith was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Seedvault VII supply manifest. ORRERY flagged Seedvault VII objective before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what resonant doors really meant.

This connects to [[operation-whisperfield|Operation Whisperfield]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[the-silent-vaults-of-rathalqu|The Silent Vaults of Rathalqu]]. Compare the account in [[hollow-spires|Hollow Spires]]. Compare the account in [[operation-paleglass-iv|Operation Paleglass IV]]. [[specialist-kenji-yilmaz|Specialist Kenji Yilmaz]] tells the other side of the story.
