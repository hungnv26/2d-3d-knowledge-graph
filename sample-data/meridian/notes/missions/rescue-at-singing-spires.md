# Rescue at Singing Spires


Rescue at Singing Spires began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest, and improvised shielding was never far from anyone's mind.

Singing Spires landing party came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Singing Spires casualty report really meant. Singing Spires supply manifest is still an open question in the archive. The lucky bolt is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the spare-parts ledger. Hull mending came up in every debrief afterwards.

This connects to [[survey-of-silent-reach|Survey of Silent Reach]] in ways that are still being argued over. This connects to [[neural-beacon|Neural beacon]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[silent-loom|Silent loom]]. See also [[orrery|ORRERY]].

**Why the singing spires was retired.** The derelict gave up its parts without complaint; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

They built what they needed out of what they found. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint.
