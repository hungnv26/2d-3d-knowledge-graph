# Rescue at Cikirush XI


The board marked Rescue at Cikirush XI succeeded in 2190, which only tells half of it. The protocol said wait, and they waited. A single prime number blinked back, and cultural firewall was never far from anyone's mind.

Cikirush XI landing party came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Cikirush XI objective before anyone else noticed it. The silent greeting came up in every debrief afterwards.
