# Operation Keystone XI


Operation Keystone XI was logged as lost in 2194. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The survey drone came back with more questions, and orbital census was never far from anyone's mind.

Keystone XI objective is still an open question in the archive. Keystone XI debrief came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on Keystone XI casualty report run to several pages. ORRERY flagged Keystone XI supply manifest before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Outer Reach. Dust rivers came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about the dark meridian the hard way.

Compare the account in [[xanves-station|Xanves Station]]. Crew who worked on [[vesny-ix-quarantine|Vesny IX Quarantine]] disagreed. [[the-glass-deeps-of-dunci|The Glass Deeps of Dunci]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[rescue-at-velvet-belt-of-harzormor|Rescue at Velvet Belt of Harzormor]]. See also [[silent-harp|Silent harp]]. [[captain-freya-quill|Captain Freya Quill]] tells the other side of the story.

**Why the keystone xi crew rotates every watch.** The survey drone came back with more questions; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

Further reading: *On Beacon Chain Decay* (Ekwueme, 2193).
