# Operation Emberline


Operation Emberline began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make, and buried observatories was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Emberline landing party before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Emberline casualty report. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Emberline debrief. Nobody agreed on what Emberline supply manifest really meant. The survey team spent a week on the Precursors alone.
