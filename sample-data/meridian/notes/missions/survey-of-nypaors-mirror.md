# Survey of Nypaor's Mirror


Survey of Nypaor's Mirror began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The charts ended and the dark began, and drift charts was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Nypaor's Mirror supply manifest before anyone else noticed it. Nypaor's Mirror landing party came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Nypaor's Mirror casualty report. The crew learned about drift charts the hard way.

The same pattern showed up at [[survey-of-selovushs-crown|Survey of Selovush's Crown]]. [[bony-weavers|Bony Weavers]] tells the other side of the story. Compare the account in [[the-amber-steppes-of-venzorka|The Amber Steppes of Venzorka]]. [[dr-signe-ekwueme|Dr. Signe Ekwueme]] tells the other side of the story.

**Why the nypaor's mirror must idle before a jump.** The survey drone came back with more questions; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The charts ended and the dark began.
