# Operation Long Echo VII


Operation Long Echo VII began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The protocol said wait, and they waited. The room held its breath for eleven minutes, and consent beacons was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Long Echo VII supply manifest before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on Long Echo VII casualty report alone. Nobody agreed on what Long Echo VII objective really meant. The crew learned about Long Echo VII landing party the hard way. Nobody agreed on what shared-zero mathematics really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to contact quarantine. The notes on the first question run to several pages.

**Why the long echo vii was retired.** Nobody spoke first, by design; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

The protocol said wait, and they waited. A single prime number blinked back.
