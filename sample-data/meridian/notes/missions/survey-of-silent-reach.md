# Survey of Silent Reach


Survey of Silent Reach began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. They built what they needed out of what they found, and structural fatigue was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Silent Reach objective really meant. ORRERY flagged Silent Reach debrief before anyone else noticed it. Silent Reach supply manifest came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Silent Reach casualty report before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about zero-g welding the hard way. The survey team spent a week on structural fatigue alone.

Crew who worked on [[watch-log-2193-274|Watch Log 2193.274]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[watch-log-2188-149|Watch Log 2188.149]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[watch-log-2194-222|Watch Log 2194.222]] disagreed. [[specialist-dmitri-zhao|Specialist Dmitri Zhao]] tells the other side of the story.

Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint.
