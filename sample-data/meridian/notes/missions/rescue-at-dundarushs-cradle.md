# Rescue at Dundarush's Cradle


Rescue at Dundarush's Cradle was logged as failed in 2192. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and spore quarantine was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Dundarush's Cradle casualty report the hard way. Dundarush's Cradle debrief is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what spore quarantine really meant.

Crew who worked on [[iron-choir|Iron Choir]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[hull-anchor|Hull anchor]]. Crew who worked on [[nypaors-mirror|Nypaor's Mirror]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[captains-log-2192-091-missing-hour|Captain's Log 2192.091 — Missing Hour]]. The same pattern showed up at [[ensign-idris-zhao|Ensign Idris Zhao]].
