# Ululmor Station Quarantine


The board marked Ululmor Station Quarantine active in 2191, which only tells half of it. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose. Every chamber faced the same distant point, and chronolith was never far from anyone's mind.

Ululmor Station supply manifest is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on Ululmor Station objective alone. The patient architecture came up in every debrief afterwards.
