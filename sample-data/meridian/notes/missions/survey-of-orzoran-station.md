# Survey of Orzoran Station


Survey of Orzoran Station was logged as planned in 2193. The serum held, barely. The fever broke on the ninth day, and spore quarantine was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Orzoran Station casualty report the hard way. The crew learned about Orzoran Station objective the hard way. Orzoran Station debrief came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about xeno-allergens the hard way. ORRERY flagged phantom symptoms before anyone else noticed it. The blue fever came up in every debrief afterwards.

The same pattern showed up at [[lattice-lens|Lattice lens]]. [[watch-log-2187-108|Watch Log 2187.108]] tells the other side of the story. Crew who worked on [[plasma-anchor|Plasma anchor]] disagreed. See also [[watch-log-2188-237|Watch Log 2188.237]]. See also [[captain-amara-zhao|Captain Amara Zhao]]. [[lieutenant-mateo-okonkwo|Lieutenant Mateo Okonkwo]] tells the other side of the story.

**Why the orzoran station was retired.** The fever broke on the ninth day; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
