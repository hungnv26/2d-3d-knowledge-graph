# Cian's Orchard Quarantine


Cian's Orchard Quarantine began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. Nobody spoke first, by design. The room held its breath for eleven minutes, and signal humility was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Cian's Orchard supply manifest. The notes on Cian's Orchard objective run to several pages. Shared-zero mathematics came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged the silent greeting before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on intent signalling alone.
