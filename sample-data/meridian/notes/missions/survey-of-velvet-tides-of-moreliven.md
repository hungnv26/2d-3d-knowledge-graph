# Survey of Velvet Tides of Moreliven


Survey of Velvet Tides of Moreliven was logged as active in 2191. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed. A gift was returned, which meant everything, and the broken promise was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Velvet Tides of Moreliven objective before anyone else noticed it. Velvet Tides of Moreliven casualty report is still an open question in the archive.
