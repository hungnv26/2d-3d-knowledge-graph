# Operation Paleglass IV


Operation Paleglass IV was logged as succeeded in 2190. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose. Every chamber faced the same distant point, and the lantern script was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Paleglass IV supply manifest really meant. The notes on Paleglass IV objective run to several pages. The survey team spent a week on star-map murals alone. Resonant doors came up in every debrief afterwards.

See also [[the-iron-fields-of-elithal|The Iron Fields of Elithal]]. The same pattern showed up at [[iron-swarm|Iron Swarm]]. See also [[operation-seedvault-ix|Operation Seedvault IX]]. Crew who worked on [[ensign-noor-quill|Ensign Noor Quill]] disagreed. See also [[dr-hana-rasmussen|Dr. Hana Rasmussen]].

**Why the paleglass iv was retired.** The builders had left, carefully, on purpose; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
