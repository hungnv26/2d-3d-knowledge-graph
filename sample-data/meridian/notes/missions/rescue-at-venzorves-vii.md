# Rescue at Venzorves VII


Rescue at Venzorves VII began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make, and spiral glyphs was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Venzorves VII supply manifest alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Venzorves VII casualty report. Venzorves VII objective is still an open question in the archive. Venzorves VII landing party came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged spiral glyphs before anyone else noticed it. Keystone engines is still an open question in the archive.
