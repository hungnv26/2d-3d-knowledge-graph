# Survey of Dundarush's Cradle


Survey of Dundarush's Cradle began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. The fever broke on the ninth day, and field surgery was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Dundarush's Cradle objective before anyone else noticed it. Dundarush's Cradle landing party came up in every debrief afterwards. Xeno-allergens came up in every debrief afterwards.

This connects to [[silent-vaults-of-cimel-quarantine|Silent Vaults of Cimel Quarantine]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[fold-lance|Fold lance]]. Crew who worked on [[crimson-grazers|Crimson Grazers]] disagreed. This connects to [[watch-log-2192-094|Watch Log 2192.094]] in ways that are still being argued over.
