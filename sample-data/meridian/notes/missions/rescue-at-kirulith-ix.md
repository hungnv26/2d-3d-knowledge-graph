# Rescue at Kirulith IX


Rescue at Kirulith IX began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. Something below the shelf answered in light. The tide came in as glass and went out as song, and the glass tide was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Kirulith IX objective the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Kirulith IX landing party. Silica shells came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged the glass tide before anyone else noticed it. The pale migration is still an open question in the archive.
