# Operation Starwell III


Operation Starwell III was logged as planned in 2194. The treaty was written in weather, not ink. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and hospitality rites was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Starwell III supply manifest alone. Nobody agreed on what Starwell III landing party really meant. ORRERY flagged Starwell III casualty report before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about trade in silence the hard way. The notes on Vell migration law run to several pages. The broken promise came up in every debrief afterwards.

The council drifted apart and together like cloud. The treaty was written in weather, not ink.
