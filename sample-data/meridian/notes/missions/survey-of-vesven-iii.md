# Survey of Vesven III


Survey of Vesven III began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make, and the Precursors was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Vesven III supply manifest before anyone else noticed it. Vesven III debrief came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vesven III landing party. Most of the arguments aboard came back to chronolith. The notes on the third inscription run to several pages.

The same pattern showed up at [[kaulaes-mirror|Kaulae's Mirror]]. See also [[operation-ninth-bell-ii|Operation Ninth Bell II]]. This connects to [[dr-elif-solberg|Dr. Elif Solberg]] in ways that are still being argued over.

**Why the vesven iii was retired.** The builders had left, carefully, on purpose; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
