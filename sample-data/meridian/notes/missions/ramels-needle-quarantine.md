# Ramel's Needle Quarantine


The board marked Ramel's Needle Quarantine active in 2189, which only tells half of it. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail, and derelict etiquette was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Ramel's Needle objective really meant. Ramel's Needle debrief came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to reactor patchwork. The notes on the lucky bolt run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the scrap economy.

This connects to [[incident-report-lantern-signal-2189|Incident Report: Lantern Signal (2189)]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[orrery|ORRERY]].
