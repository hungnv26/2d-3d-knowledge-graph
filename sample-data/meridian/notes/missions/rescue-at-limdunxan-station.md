# Rescue at Limdunxan Station


The board marked Rescue at Limdunxan Station succeeded in 2193, which only tells half of it. The council drifted apart and together like cloud. The treaty was written in weather, not ink, and the Vell was never far from anyone's mind.

Limdunxan Station casualty report is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged Limdunxan Station landing party before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vell honour debts. The notes on Vell migration law run to several pages.

Compare the account in [[ormors-bell-quarantine|Ormor's Bell Quarantine]]. Crew who worked on [[sleeping-kelp|Sleeping Kelp]] disagreed. See also [[chief-mateo-quill|Chief Mateo Quill]].
