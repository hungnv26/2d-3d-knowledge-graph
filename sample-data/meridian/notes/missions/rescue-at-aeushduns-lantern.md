# Rescue at Aeushdun's Lantern


Rescue at Aeushdun's Lantern was logged as succeeded in 2188. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone. The serum held, barely, and cryo triage was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Aeushdun's Lantern objective really meant. Nobody agreed on what Aeushdun's Lantern debrief really meant. The notes on Aeushdun's Lantern supply manifest run to several pages. The crew learned about Aeushdun's Lantern landing party the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to decontamination arch. The survey team spent a week on bone-density drift alone. The crew learned about xeno-allergens the hard way.

The serum held, barely. The fever broke on the ninth day.
