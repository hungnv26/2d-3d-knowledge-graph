# Survey of Anushkir Station


Survey of Anushkir Station was logged as succeeded in 2192. Something below the shelf answered in light. The water glowed a patient blue, and reef memory was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Anushkir Station supply manifest before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Anushkir Station objective. The pale migration is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the glass tide.

See also [[singing-deeps|Singing Deeps]]. Compare the account in [[operation-quiet-harbour-vi|Operation Quiet Harbour VI]]. The same pattern showed up at [[nypaors-mirror-quarantine|Nypaor's Mirror Quarantine]]. Compare the account in [[specialist-leilani-castellanos|Specialist Leilani Castellanos]]. This connects to [[lieutenant-leilani-delacroix|Lieutenant Leilani Delacroix]] in ways that are still being argued over.

**Why the anushkir station was retired.** The tide came in as glass and went out as song; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
