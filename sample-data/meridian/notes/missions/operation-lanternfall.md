# Operation Lanternfall


Operation Lanternfall began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. Samples sang faintly in their jars. Something below the shelf answered in light, and bioluminescent grammar was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Lanternfall supply manifest run to several pages. Lanternfall debrief is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged bioluminescent grammar before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about the pale migration the hard way.

[[echo-abacus|Echo abacus]] tells the other side of the story. See also [[tethtethlim-ix-quarantine|Tethtethlim IX Quarantine]]. Crew who worked on [[cira-iii|Cira III]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[broken-shoals|Broken Shoals]]. Compare the account in [[the-silent-vaults-of-cimel|The Silent Vaults of Cimel]]. Crew who worked on [[dr-hana-quill|Dr. Hana Quill]] disagreed.

The tide came in as glass and went out as song. Something below the shelf answered in light.
