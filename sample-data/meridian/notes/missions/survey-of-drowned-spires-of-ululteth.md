# Survey of Drowned Spires of Ululteth


Survey of Drowned Spires of Ululteth began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. Every chamber faced the same distant point, and harmonic masonry was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Drowned Spires of Ululteth objective before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Drowned Spires of Ululteth debrief. The survey team spent a week on Drowned Spires of Ululteth casualty report alone. The survey team spent a week on Drowned Spires of Ululteth supply manifest alone. Nobody agreed on what ossuary gardens really meant.

**Why the drowned spires of ululteth is kept behind two doors.** Every chamber faced the same distant point; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
