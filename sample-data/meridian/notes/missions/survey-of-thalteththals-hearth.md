# Survey of Thalteththal's Hearth


Survey of Thalteththal's Hearth began, like most missions, with a bad map and good intentions. They built what they needed out of what they found. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest, and salvage rights was never far from anyone's mind.

Thalteththal's Hearth casualty report is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on Thalteththal's Hearth debrief alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Thalteththal's Hearth supply manifest. Nobody agreed on what the engineer’s oath really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the lucky bolt. The notes on the scrap economy run to several pages.
