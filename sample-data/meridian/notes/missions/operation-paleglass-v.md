# Operation Paleglass V


The board marked Operation Paleglass V planned in 2194, which only tells half of it. The tide came in as glass and went out as song. Something below the shelf answered in light, and the pale migration was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Paleglass V objective really meant. Paleglass V casualty report came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on Paleglass V debrief alone. Nobody agreed on what Paleglass V landing party really meant. Nobody agreed on what bioluminescent grammar really meant.
