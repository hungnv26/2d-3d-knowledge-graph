# Lattice sail


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Lattice sail. Every jump left a faint scar in the charts. The resonance needle climbed past amber, and fold harmonics was never far from anyone's mind.

Lattice sail field test came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Lattice sail prototype. The notes on Lattice sail failure mode run to several pages. ORRERY flagged fold scarring before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on the quiet jump alone.

See also [[spore-beacon|Spore beacon]]. Crew who worked on [[limdunxan-station|Limdunxan Station]] disagreed. Compare the account in [[watch-log-2187-048|Watch Log 2187.048]]. Crew who worked on [[ormors-bell-quarantine|Ormor's Bell Quarantine]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[orrery|ORRERY]].

**Why the lattice sail was retired.** The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

Every jump left a faint scar in the charts. Space folded like wet paper.
