# Plasma mender


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Plasma mender. The telemetry told a calmer story than the crew. ORRERY logged the decision and, unusually, its doubt, and the orrery dream was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Plasma mender power draw. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Plasma mender calibration. Plasma mender field test came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the ninth fallback. The notes on the trust ledger run to several pages.

This connects to [[commander-sofia-nakamura|Commander Sofia Nakamura]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[tidal-mender|Tidal mender]] disagreed.
