# Graviton drive


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Graviton drive. The serum held, barely. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and contagion modelling was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Graviton drive failure mode really meant. Graviton drive prototype came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on Graviton drive power draw alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the isolation ward. The crew learned about cryo triage the hard way.

**Why the graviton drive was retired.** Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
