# Graviton abacus


The Graviton abacus was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Water was the only currency that mattered. Every leaf was counted twice, and emergency sprouting was never far from anyone's mind.

Graviton abacus safety interlock came up in every debrief afterwards. Graviton abacus calibration came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged nutrient cascade before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on the salt blight alone.

**Why the graviton abacus is kept behind two doors.** The harvest came in thin but alive; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
