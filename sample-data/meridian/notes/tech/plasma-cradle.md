# Plasma cradle


The Plasma cradle is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. The fever broke on the ninth day. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and field surgery was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Plasma cradle failure mode run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Plasma cradle power draw. The notes on contagion modelling run to several pages. ORRERY flagged phantom symptoms before anyone else noticed it.

See also [[specialist-zainab-solberg|Specialist Zainab Solberg]]. [[rescue-at-dundarushs-cradle|Rescue at Dundarush's Cradle]] tells the other side of the story.
