# Neural drive


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Neural drive. Every jump left a faint scar in the charts. The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance, and the Adeyemi limit was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Neural drive safety interlock really meant. The notes on Neural drive prototype run to several pages. The crew learned about Neural drive failure mode the hard way. Nobody agreed on what harmonic lock really meant. Exotic matter ballast came up in every debrief afterwards.
