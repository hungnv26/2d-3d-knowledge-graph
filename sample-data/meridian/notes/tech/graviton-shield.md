# Graviton shield


The Graviton shield is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. Water was the only currency that mattered. Every leaf was counted twice, and the salt blight was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Graviton shield failure mode alone. The survey team spent a week on Graviton shield field test alone. Graviton shield prototype is still an open question in the archive. The notes on nutrient cascade run to several pages. ORRERY flagged oxygen garden before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what mycelium bricks really meant.
