# Ion crystal


The Ion crystal was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. They built what they needed out of what they found, and zero-g welding was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ion crystal failure mode. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ion crystal field test. The crew learned about Ion crystal prototype the hard way. Nobody agreed on what reactor patchwork really meant. ORRERY flagged field fabrication before anyone else noticed it.

Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail.
