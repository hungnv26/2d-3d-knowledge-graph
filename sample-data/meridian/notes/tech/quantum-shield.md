# Quantum shield


The Quantum shield is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. The fever broke on the ninth day. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and the isolation ward was never far from anyone's mind.

Quantum shield failure mode came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about Quantum shield safety interlock the hard way. The notes on Quantum shield power draw run to several pages. The crew learned about cryo triage the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to long-sleep recovery. Nobody agreed on what xeno-allergens really meant.

Further reading: *Cryo Triage Outcomes* (Brightwater, 2189).

Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. The serum held, barely.
