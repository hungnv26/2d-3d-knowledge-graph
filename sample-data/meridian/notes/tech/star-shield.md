# Star shield


The Star shield was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail, and derelict etiquette was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Star shield prototype really meant. The crew learned about Star shield power draw the hard way. The survey team spent a week on Star shield calibration alone. Nobody agreed on what Star shield failure mode really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to structural fatigue. ORRERY flagged improvised shielding before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about the spare-parts ledger the hard way.
