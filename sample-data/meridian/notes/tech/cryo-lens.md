# Cryo lens


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Cryo lens. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail, and zero-g welding was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Cryo lens calibration. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Cryo lens power draw. ORRERY flagged the lucky bolt before anyone else noticed it. The notes on the spare-parts ledger run to several pages.

The same pattern showed up at [[watch-log-2188-238|Watch Log 2188.238]]. See also [[cryo-beacon|Cryo beacon]]. See also [[lieutenant-leilani-delacroix|Lieutenant Leilani Delacroix]]. Crew who worked on [[specialist-oskar-lindqvist|Specialist Oskar Lindqvist]] disagreed. See also [[incident-report-lantern-signal-2189|Incident Report: Lantern Signal (2189)]]. The same pattern showed up at [[specialist-noor-ekwueme|Specialist Noor Ekwueme]]. The same pattern showed up at [[survey-of-velvet-vaults-of-zorkirhar|Survey of Velvet Vaults of Zorkirhar]].

Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. They built what they needed out of what they found.
