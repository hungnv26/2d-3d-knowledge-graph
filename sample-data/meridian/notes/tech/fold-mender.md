# Fold mender


The Fold mender was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and the immunity map was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Fold mender prototype the hard way. The survey team spent a week on Fold mender calibration alone. The notes on long-sleep recovery run to several pages.

Further reading: *Xeno-Allergen Screening* (Adeyemi, 2192).

Further reading: *On Cryo Triage Outcomes* (Carrow, 2183).
