# Silent harp


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Silent harp. They built what they needed out of what they found. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest, and derelict etiquette was never far from anyone's mind.

Silent harp calibration is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what Silent harp failure mode really meant. ORRERY flagged the engineer’s oath before anyone else noticed it. The notes on zero-g welding run to several pages.

See also [[the-silent-vaults-of-cimel|The Silent Vaults of Cimel]]. Crew who worked on [[lattice-cradle|Lattice cradle]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[ramels-needle-quarantine|Ramel's Needle Quarantine]]. The same pattern showed up at [[limthalven-station-quarantine|Limthalven Station Quarantine]]. Compare the account in [[rescue-at-singing-reach-of-dundunhar|Rescue at Singing Reach of Dundunhar]].

**Why the silent harp was retired.** Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
