# Lattice loom


The Lattice loom is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail, and field fabrication was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Lattice loom calibration before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged Lattice loom power draw before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Lattice loom failure mode. The notes on Lattice loom prototype run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the lucky bolt.

Crew who worked on [[lieutenant-mateo-valde-s|Lieutenant Mateo Valdés]] disagreed. See also [[silent-harp|Silent harp]].

**Why the lattice loom is never run unattended.** They built what they needed out of what they found; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
