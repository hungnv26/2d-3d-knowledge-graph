# Lattice abacus


The Lattice abacus was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. They built what they needed out of what they found. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest, and the scrap economy was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Lattice abacus calibration before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what Lattice abacus failure mode really meant. The survey team spent a week on field fabrication alone. The notes on reactor patchwork run to several pages.

Compare the account in [[survey-of-vesves-vii|Survey of Vesves VII]]. See also [[hull-shield|Hull shield]]. [[operation-quiet-harbour-vii|Operation Quiet Harbour VII]] tells the other side of the story. This connects to [[captains-log-2187-277-vell-gift|Captain's Log 2187.277 — Vell Gift]] in ways that are still being argued over.

The hull held because someone refused to let it fail. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest.
