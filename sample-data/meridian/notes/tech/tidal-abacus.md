# Tidal abacus


The Tidal abacus was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail, and zero-g welding was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Tidal abacus failure mode the hard way. Tidal abacus field test came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on Tidal abacus calibration run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what salvage rights really meant. ORRERY flagged improvised shielding before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged the spare-parts ledger before anyone else noticed it.

The same pattern showed up at [[lattice-loom|Lattice loom]]. See also [[commander-signe-vantablack|Commander Signe Vantablack]]. Crew who worked on [[incident-report-lantern-signal-2189|Incident Report: Lantern Signal (2189)]] disagreed. See also [[specialist-oskar-lindqvist|Specialist Oskar Lindqvist]].

The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. They built what they needed out of what they found.
