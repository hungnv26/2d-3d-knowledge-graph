# Star lens


The Star lens was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. The serum held, barely. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and phantom symptoms was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Star lens calibration really meant. The notes on Star lens prototype run to several pages. ORRERY flagged Star lens power draw before anyone else noticed it.
