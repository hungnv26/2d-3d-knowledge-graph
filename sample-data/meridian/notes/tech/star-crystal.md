# Star crystal


The Star crystal is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and beacon chains was never far from anyone's mind.

Star crystal prototype came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Star crystal field test really meant. The survey team spent a week on Star crystal failure mode alone. Star crystal power draw is still an open question in the archive. The crew learned about the dark meridian the hard way.

See also [[ion-beacon|Ion beacon]]. See also [[quantum-scrubber|Quantum scrubber]]. This connects to [[raovven-station|Raovven Station]] in ways that are still being argued over. This connects to [[silent-loom|Silent loom]] in ways that are still being argued over.

**Why the star crystal was retired.** The charts ended and the dark began; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
