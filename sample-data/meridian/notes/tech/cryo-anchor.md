# Cryo anchor


The Cryo anchor was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. The green deck smelled of rain that never fell. The harvest came in thin but alive, and nutrient cascade was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Cryo anchor failure mode alone. ORRERY flagged Cryo anchor calibration before anyone else noticed it. Cryo anchor power draw is still an open question in the archive. Cryo anchor prototype came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about mycelium bricks the hard way.
