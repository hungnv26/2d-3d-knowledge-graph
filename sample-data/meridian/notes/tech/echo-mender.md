# Echo mender


The Echo mender was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Space folded like wet paper. The resonance needle climbed past amber, and tachyon shear was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Echo mender field test. The crew learned about Echo mender prototype the hard way. The survey team spent a week on causality buffer alone.
