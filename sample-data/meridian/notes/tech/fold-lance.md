# Fold lance


The Fold lance is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. The serum held, barely, and phantom symptoms was never far from anyone's mind.

Fold lance calibration came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Fold lance power draw before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what Fold lance failure mode really meant. Cryo triage is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what xeno-allergens really meant. The crew learned about the blue fever the hard way.

The same pattern showed up at [[singing-crawlers|Singing Crawlers]]. The same pattern showed up at [[operation-keystone|Operation Keystone]].

Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone. The serum held, barely.
