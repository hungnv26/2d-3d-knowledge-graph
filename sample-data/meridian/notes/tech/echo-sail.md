# Echo sail


The Echo sail was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Water was the only currency that mattered. The green deck smelled of rain that never fell, and oxygen garden was never far from anyone's mind.

Echo sail prototype is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what Echo sail field test really meant. The survey team spent a week on Echo sail failure mode alone. The survey team spent a week on nutrient cascade alone. ORRERY flagged the last tomato before anyone else noticed it.

[[neural-compass|Neural compass]] tells the other side of the story. Crew who worked on [[lattice-anchor|Lattice anchor]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[cryo-lance|Cryo lance]]. Crew who worked on [[incident-report-lantern-signal-2191|Incident Report: Lantern Signal (2191)]] disagreed.

The green deck smelled of rain that never fell. The harvest came in thin but alive.
