# Hull anchor


The Hull anchor was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. The serum held, barely, and phantom symptoms was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Hull anchor power draw. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Hull anchor prototype. The crew learned about Hull anchor failure mode the hard way. The survey team spent a week on long-sleep recovery alone. Contagion modelling is still an open question in the archive. The notes on the blue fever run to several pages.

This connects to [[amber-crawlers|Amber Crawlers]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[cryo-shield|Cryo shield]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[watch-log-2192-210|Watch Log 2192.210]] disagreed. See also [[star-lens|Star lens]]. The same pattern showed up at [[lattice-lens|Lattice lens]].
