# Lattice compass


The Lattice compass is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. They built what they needed out of what they found. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail, and field fabrication was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Lattice compass calibration really meant. Nobody agreed on what Lattice compass field test really meant. ORRERY flagged Lattice compass failure mode before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Lattice compass prototype. Zero-g welding came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what the cannibal refit really meant. The survey team spent a week on stress fractures alone.

The same pattern showed up at [[specialist-oskar-lindqvist|Specialist Oskar Lindqvist]]. [[tidal-crystal|Tidal crystal]] tells the other side of the story. [[burning-bloom|Burning Bloom]] tells the other side of the story. [[rescue-at-thalselthal-xi|Rescue at Thalselthal XI]] tells the other side of the story. Crew who worked on [[specialist-noor-ekwueme|Specialist Noor Ekwueme]] disagreed.

**Why the lattice compass is kept behind two doors.** They built what they needed out of what they found; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail.
