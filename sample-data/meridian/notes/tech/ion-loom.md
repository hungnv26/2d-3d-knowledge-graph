# Ion loom


The Ion loom is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest, and the junkyard fleet was never far from anyone's mind.

Ion loom prototype came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Ion loom safety interlock really meant. The engineer’s oath is still an open question in the archive.

**Why the ion loom protocol waits eleven minutes.** The hull held because someone refused to let it fail; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

They built what they needed out of what they found. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint.
