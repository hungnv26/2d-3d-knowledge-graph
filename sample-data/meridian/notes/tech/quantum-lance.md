# Quantum lance


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Quantum lance. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail. They built what they needed out of what they found, and stress fractures was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Quantum lance prototype. The crew learned about Quantum lance calibration the hard way. Structural fatigue is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged the engineer’s oath before anyone else noticed it.

This connects to [[operation-ninth-bell-ii|Operation Ninth Bell II]] in ways that are still being argued over. [[silent-harp|Silent harp]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[specialist-oskar-lindqvist|Specialist Oskar Lindqvist]]. [[cryo-lens|Cryo lens]] tells the other side of the story. See also [[spore-shield|Spore shield]]. Compare the account in [[rescue-at-keplers-lantern|Rescue at Kepler's Lantern]].
