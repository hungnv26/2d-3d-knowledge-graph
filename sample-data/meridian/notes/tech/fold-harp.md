# Fold harp


The Fold harp is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. Somewhere in the core a subroutine hesitated. The telemetry told a calmer story than the crew, and the soft override was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Fold harp safety interlock the hard way. Fold harp failure mode came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Fold harp calibration before anyone else noticed it. Fold harp field test is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on the quiet subroutine alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to ORRERY.

See also [[tidal-mender|Tidal mender]]. [[incident-report-fold-stall-2189|Incident Report: Fold Stall (2189)]] tells the other side of the story. See also [[plasma-mender|Plasma mender]]. This connects to [[orrery|ORRERY]] in ways that are still being argued over.
