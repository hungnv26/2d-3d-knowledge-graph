# Ion furnace


The Ion furnace was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone. The serum held, barely, and spore quarantine was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Ion furnace safety interlock alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ion furnace power draw. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ion furnace prototype. The survey team spent a week on contagion modelling alone.
