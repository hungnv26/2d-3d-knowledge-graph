# Lattice lens


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Lattice lens. The fever broke on the ninth day. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and phantom symptoms was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Lattice lens calibration the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Lattice lens power draw. The notes on Lattice lens field test run to several pages. ORRERY flagged Lattice lens failure mode before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what the blue fever really meant.
