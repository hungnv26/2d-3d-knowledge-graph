# Quantum compass


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Quantum compass. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. The serum held, barely, and the isolation ward was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Quantum compass failure mode. The survey team spent a week on Quantum compass calibration alone. ORRERY flagged Quantum compass safety interlock before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what spore quarantine really meant.
