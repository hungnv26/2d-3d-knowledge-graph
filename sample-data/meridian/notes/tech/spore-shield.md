# Spore shield


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Spore shield. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail. They built what they needed out of what they found, and zero-g welding was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Spore shield power draw really meant. ORRERY flagged Spore shield calibration before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Spore shield prototype. Structural fatigue came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged hull mending before anyone else noticed it.

[[tidal-abacus|Tidal abacus]] tells the other side of the story. See also [[star-shield|Star shield]]. This connects to [[captains-log-2187-277-vell-gift|Captain's Log 2187.277 — Vell Gift]] in ways that are still being argued over. [[commander-signe-vantablack|Commander Signe Vantablack]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[rescue-at-singing-spires|Rescue at Singing Spires]].

Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint.
