# Tidal beacon


The Tidal beacon was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. The serum held, barely. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and spore quarantine was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Tidal beacon field test. Nobody agreed on what Tidal beacon prototype really meant. ORRERY flagged the blue fever before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the immunity map. ORRERY flagged cryo triage before anyone else noticed it.
