# Tidal crystal


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Tidal crystal. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail. They built what they needed out of what they found, and reactor patchwork was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Tidal crystal failure mode alone. Nobody agreed on what Tidal crystal calibration really meant. Tidal crystal safety interlock came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Tidal crystal prototype before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged the engineer’s oath before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged salvage rights before anyone else noticed it.
