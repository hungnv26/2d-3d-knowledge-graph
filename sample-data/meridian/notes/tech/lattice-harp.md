# Lattice harp


The Lattice harp is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. Space folded like wet paper. The resonance needle climbed past amber, and the Adeyemi limit was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Lattice harp power draw before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Lattice harp failure mode. The crew learned about tachyon shear the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to drive bloom.

[[captains-log-2191-179-vell-gift|Captain's Log 2191.179 — Vell Gift]] tells the other side of the story. Compare the account in [[hull-compass|Hull compass]]. Crew who worked on [[tidal-cradle|Tidal cradle]] disagreed.

**Why the lattice harp protocol waits eleven minutes.** The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
