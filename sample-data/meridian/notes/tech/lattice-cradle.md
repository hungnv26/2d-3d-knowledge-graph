# Lattice cradle


The Lattice cradle was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. They built what they needed out of what they found, and hull mending was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Lattice cradle field test run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Lattice cradle power draw. The lucky bolt is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the scrap economy.
