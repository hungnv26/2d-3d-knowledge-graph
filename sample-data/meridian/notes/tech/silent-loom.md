# Silent loom


The Silent loom is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. The charts ended and the dark began. The map was redrawn for the ninth time, and parallax ladders was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Silent loom power draw the hard way. ORRERY flagged Silent loom failure mode before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on Silent loom field test alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to uncharted silence. Gravity shoals is still an open question in the archive. Star-fall came up in every debrief afterwards.
