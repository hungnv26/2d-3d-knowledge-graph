# Star drive


The Star drive is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four. The map was redrawn for the ninth time, and the long survey was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Star drive power draw. Nobody agreed on what Star drive calibration really meant. Star drive failure mode came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on parallax ladders alone. Orbital census came up in every debrief afterwards.

This connects to [[operation-lanternfall-iv|Operation Lanternfall IV]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[venthals-orchard|Venthal's Orchard]]. Compare the account in [[orrery|ORRERY]].
