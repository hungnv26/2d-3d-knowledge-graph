# Echo shield


The Echo shield is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. Every jump left a faint scar in the charts. Space folded like wet paper, and tachyon shear was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Echo shield calibration alone. Echo shield safety interlock is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what Echo shield prototype really meant. The crew learned about Echo shield field test the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to harmonic lock.

This connects to [[nyriss-cradle|Nyris's Cradle]] in ways that are still being argued over. [[the-singing-deeps-of-aesel|The Singing Deeps of Aesel]] tells the other side of the story.

Further reading: *On Tachyon Shear in Crewed Jumps* (Moreau, 2186).

The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance. The resonance needle climbed past amber.
