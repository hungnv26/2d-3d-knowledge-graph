# Graviton beacon


The Graviton beacon was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. The fever broke on the ninth day. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and the isolation ward was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Graviton beacon prototype alone. Graviton beacon power draw came up in every debrief afterwards. Contagion modelling is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what xeno-allergens really meant.

The same pattern showed up at [[ramels-needle|Ramel's Needle]]. See also [[silent-vaults-of-cimel-quarantine|Silent Vaults of Cimel Quarantine]]. See also [[amber-moss|Amber Moss]].
