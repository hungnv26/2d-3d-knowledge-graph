# Tidal lance


The Tidal lance is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. The hull held because someone refused to let it fail. They built what they needed out of what they found, and zero-g welding was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Tidal lance failure mode really meant. ORRERY flagged Tidal lance safety interlock before anyone else noticed it. Derelict etiquette came up in every debrief afterwards.

**Why the tidal lance was retired.** Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

They built what they needed out of what they found. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest.
