# Silent drive


The Silent drive is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four. The survey drone came back with more questions, and star-fall was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Silent drive power draw really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Silent drive failure mode. ORRERY flagged Silent drive safety interlock before anyone else noticed it. The notes on Silent drive prototype run to several pages. ORRERY flagged the cartographer’s error before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about waypoint decay the hard way.

Compare the account in [[limthalven-station|Limthalven Station]]. This connects to [[xananeli-station-quarantine|Xananeli Station Quarantine]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[vesves-vii|Vesves VII]].

**Why the silent drive was retired.** The charts ended and the dark began; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The survey drone came back with more questions.
