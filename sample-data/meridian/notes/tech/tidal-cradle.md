# Tidal cradle


The Tidal cradle was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. The green deck smelled of rain that never fell. Every leaf was counted twice, and the last tomato was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Tidal cradle power draw before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about Tidal cradle safety interlock the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the last tomato. The crew learned about oxygen garden the hard way.
