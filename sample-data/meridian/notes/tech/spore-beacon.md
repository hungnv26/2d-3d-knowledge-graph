# Spore beacon


Every engineer aboard has an opinion about the Spore beacon. The harvest came in thin but alive. Water was the only currency that mattered, and the last tomato was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Spore beacon prototype run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what Spore beacon calibration really meant. The crew learned about Spore beacon field test the hard way. The survey team spent a week on the long harvest alone. Nobody agreed on what light starvation really meant. Root rot crisis came up in every debrief afterwards.

Crew who worked on [[cryo-lance|Cryo lance]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[crimson-weavers|Crimson Weavers]] disagreed. [[tidal-harp|Tidal harp]] tells the other side of the story.

**Why the spore beacon was retired.** Every leaf was counted twice; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.

The green deck smelled of rain that never fell. Water was the only currency that mattered.
