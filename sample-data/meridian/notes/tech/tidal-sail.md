# Tidal sail


The Tidal sail is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. Water was the only currency that mattered. The green deck smelled of rain that never fell, and rationing ladder was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Tidal sail failure mode before anyone else noticed it. Tidal sail calibration came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what oxygen garden really meant. Nobody agreed on what the green deck really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to light starvation.

The same pattern showed up at [[ushnyci-leviathans|Ushnyci Leviathans]]. See also [[singing-swarm|Singing Swarm]]. This connects to [[cryo-lance|Cryo lance]] in ways that are still being argued over. This connects to [[rescue-at-nyzorkirs-mirror|Rescue at Nyzorkir's Mirror]] in ways that are still being argued over.
