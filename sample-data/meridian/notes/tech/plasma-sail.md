# Plasma sail


The Plasma sail was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and the sickbay ledger was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Plasma sail safety interlock. The notes on Plasma sail calibration run to several pages. Plasma sail field test is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged Plasma sail power draw before anyone else noticed it. The immunity map came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on the isolation ward run to several pages.

Compare the account in [[lieutenant-elif-takeda|Lieutenant Elif Takeda]]. Crew who worked on [[xanlimvens-veil|Xanlimven's Veil]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[hull-scrubber|Hull scrubber]] disagreed. This connects to [[orrery|ORRERY]] in ways that are still being argued over.

**Why the plasma sail must idle before a jump.** The serum held, barely; the crew learned this the hard way, and wrote it down so the next crew would not have to.
