# Echo compass


The Echo compass is the kind of machine that works perfectly until it becomes a story. The serum held, barely. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and the blue fever was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Echo compass safety interlock. The notes on Echo compass calibration run to several pages. The Halvorsen serum is still an open question in the archive.

[[dr-elif-solberg|Dr. Elif Solberg]] tells the other side of the story. Crew who worked on [[graviton-beacon|Graviton beacon]] disagreed.

Further reading: *On The Blue Fever Outbreak* (Delacroix, 2181).

Further reading: *Notes on Xeno-Allergen Screening* (Solberg, 2188).
