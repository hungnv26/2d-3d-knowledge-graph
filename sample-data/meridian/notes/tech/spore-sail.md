# Spore sail


The Spore sail was fitted during the second refit and never fully trusted. Space folded like wet paper. The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance, and jump echo was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Spore sail safety interlock before anyone else noticed it. Spore sail field test came up in every debrief afterwards. Spore sail power draw came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Spore sail prototype. The survey team spent a week on harmonic lock alone. The crew learned about tachyon shear the hard way.
