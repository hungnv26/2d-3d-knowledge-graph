# Drowned Rays


The Drowned Rays were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. A gift was returned, which meant everything. The treaty was written in weather, not ink, and shared-sky accord was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Drowned Rays venom alone. ORRERY flagged Drowned Rays symbiosis before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged Drowned Rays nesting grounds before anyone else noticed it. The returned gift came up in every debrief afterwards. The drifter council is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the slow treaty.
