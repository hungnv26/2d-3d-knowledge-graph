# Pale Lanterns


Pale Lanterns are loud to watch and harder to classify. The tide came in as glass and went out as song. Samples sang faintly in their jars, and current-walking was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Pale Lanterns life cycle really meant. Pale Lanterns nesting grounds came up in every debrief afterwards. Pale Lanterns courtship lights came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about Pale Lanterns song the hard way. Silica shells is still an open question in the archive.
