# Glass Shoals


The archive keeps more questions than answers about the Glass Shoals. The fever broke on the ninth day. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and the sickbay ledger was never far from anyone's mind.

Glass Shoals courtship lights came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on Glass Shoals symbiosis alone. The crew learned about Glass Shoals nesting grounds the hard way. Nobody agreed on what field surgery really meant. Nobody agreed on what xeno-allergens really meant.
