# Crimson Grazers


Crimson Grazers are lonely to watch and harder to classify. Samples sang faintly in their jars. Something below the shelf answered in light, and tidal symbiosis was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Crimson Grazers nesting grounds. Crimson Grazers symbiosis came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what reef memory really meant. ORRERY flagged the pale migration before anyone else noticed it.
