# Burning Kelp


The Burning Kelp were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The council drifted apart and together like cloud. The treaty was written in weather, not ink, and the drifter council was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Burning Kelp song. ORRERY flagged Burning Kelp nesting grounds before anyone else noticed it. The notes on wind-reading run to several pages.

Compare the account in [[venor-swarm|Venor Swarm]]. The same pattern showed up at [[sleeping-kelp|Sleeping Kelp]]. [[frozen-wastes|Frozen Wastes]] tells the other side of the story. [[operation-emberline-ix|Operation Emberline IX]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[watch-log-2194-157|Watch Log 2194.157]].

Further reading: *On Drift-Speech Phonology* (Solberg, 2192).
