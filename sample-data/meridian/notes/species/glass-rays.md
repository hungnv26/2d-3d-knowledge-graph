# Glass Rays


The Glass Rays were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. Water was the only currency that mattered. The green deck smelled of rain that never fell, and light starvation was never far from anyone's mind.

Glass Rays nesting grounds came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about Glass Rays migration the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to rationing ladder. The long harvest is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on compost engine alone.
