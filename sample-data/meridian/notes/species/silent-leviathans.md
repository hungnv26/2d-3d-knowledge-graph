# Silent Leviathans


Silent Leviathans are unsettling to watch and harder to classify. The protocol said wait, and they waited. A single prime number blinked back, and gesture grammar was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Silent Leviathans song before anyone else noticed it. Silent Leviathans life cycle came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Silent Leviathans nesting grounds really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the silent greeting. ORRERY flagged contact quarantine before anyone else noticed it. The notes on shared-zero mathematics run to several pages.

Compare the account in [[hungry-choir|Hungry Choir]]. This connects to [[thalushs-harbour|Thalush's Harbour]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[specialist-kenji-yilmaz|Specialist Kenji Yilmaz]] disagreed. Compare the account in [[graviton-shield|Graviton shield]]. The same pattern showed up at [[frozen-drifters|Frozen Drifters]]. This connects to [[orrery|ORRERY]] in ways that are still being argued over.

The protocol said wait, and they waited. Nobody spoke first, by design.
