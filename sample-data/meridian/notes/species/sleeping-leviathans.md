# Sleeping Leviathans


The Sleeping Leviathans were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. Something below the shelf answered in light. The tide came in as glass and went out as song, and reef memory was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Sleeping Leviathans courtship lights the hard way. Sleeping Leviathans migration came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what the glass tide really meant. The survey team spent a week on lantern chemistry alone.
