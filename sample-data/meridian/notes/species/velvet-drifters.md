# Velvet Drifters


The Velvet Drifters were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The tide came in as glass and went out as song. Something below the shelf answered in light, and the pale migration was never far from anyone's mind.

Velvet Drifters migration is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged Velvet Drifters nesting grounds before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what Velvet Drifters life cycle really meant. Nobody agreed on what pressure bloom really meant. Nobody agreed on what spore-song really meant.
