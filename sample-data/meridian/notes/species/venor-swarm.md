# Venor Swarm


The Venor Swarm were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The tide came in as glass and went out as song. Samples sang faintly in their jars, and the drowning light was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Venor Swarm song the hard way. Venor Swarm venom came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on Venor Swarm symbiosis alone. Venor Swarm courtship lights is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what tidal symbiosis really meant. Spore-song came up in every debrief afterwards. Lantern chemistry came up in every debrief afterwards.

This connects to [[operation-glasswing|Operation Glasswing]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[nypaors-mirror-quarantine|Nypaor's Mirror Quarantine]]. Crew who worked on [[navigator-bao-achterberg|Navigator Bao Achterberg]] disagreed.

Samples sang faintly in their jars. Something below the shelf answered in light.
