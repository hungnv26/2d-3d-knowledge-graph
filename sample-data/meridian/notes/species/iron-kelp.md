# Iron Kelp


Iron Kelp are unsettling to watch and harder to classify. The treaty was written in weather, not ink. A gift was returned, which meant everything, and Vell honour debts was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Iron Kelp song alone. The notes on Iron Kelp venom run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what hospitality rites really meant.
