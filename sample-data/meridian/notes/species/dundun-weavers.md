# Dundun Weavers


The Dundun Weavers were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The treaty was written in weather, not ink. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and the drifter council was never far from anyone's mind.

Dundun Weavers migration is still an open question in the archive. The crew learned about Dundun Weavers life cycle the hard way. The survey team spent a week on Dundun Weavers symbiosis alone. ORRERY flagged Dundun Weavers nesting grounds before anyone else noticed it. Hospitality rites is still an open question in the archive.
