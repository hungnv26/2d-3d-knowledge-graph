# Drowned Wardens


Drowned Wardens are lonely to watch and harder to classify. The council drifted apart and together like cloud. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and the slow treaty was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Drowned Wardens venom really meant. The survey team spent a week on Drowned Wardens song alone. Nobody agreed on what Drowned Wardens symbiosis really meant. Drift-speech came up in every debrief afterwards. The gift exchange is still an open question in the archive.

This connects to [[rescue-at-nyzorkirs-mirror|Rescue at Nyzorkir's Mirror]] in ways that are still being argued over. [[ululmor-station|Ululmor Station]] tells the other side of the story. Crew who worked on [[frozen-shoals|Frozen Shoals]] disagreed.
