# Silent Drifters


Silent Drifters are generous to watch and harder to classify. The council drifted apart and together like cloud. The treaty was written in weather, not ink, and the gift exchange was never far from anyone's mind.

Silent Drifters song is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what Silent Drifters life cycle really meant. ORRERY flagged Silent Drifters nesting grounds before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about Silent Drifters symbiosis the hard way. The survey team spent a week on the drifter council alone.

The treaty was written in weather, not ink. A gift was returned, which meant everything.
