# Burning Moss


Burning Moss are impossible to watch and harder to classify. The treaty was written in weather, not ink. A gift was returned, which meant everything, and the returned gift was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Burning Moss symbiosis before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about Burning Moss nesting grounds the hard way. The crew learned about trade in silence the hard way.
