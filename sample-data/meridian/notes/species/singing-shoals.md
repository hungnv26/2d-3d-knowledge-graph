# Singing Shoals


The Singing Shoals were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone. The fever broke on the ninth day, and the blue fever was never far from anyone's mind.

Singing Shoals courtship lights came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on Singing Shoals symbiosis run to several pages. The survey team spent a week on Singing Shoals migration alone. Phantom symptoms is still an open question in the archive. Xeno-allergens came up in every debrief afterwards.

The same pattern showed up at [[silent-wardens|Silent Wardens]]. Compare the account in [[captains-log-2192-091-missing-hour|Captain's Log 2192.091 — Missing Hour]]. [[star-lens|Star lens]] tells the other side of the story. [[cryo-crystal|Cryo crystal]] tells the other side of the story.

Further reading: *On Xeno-Allergen Screening* (Oyelaran, 2190).
