# Drowned Crawlers


The Drowned Crawlers were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The water glowed a patient blue. Samples sang faintly in their jars, and the drowning light was never far from anyone's mind.

Drowned Crawlers symbiosis came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about Drowned Crawlers song the hard way. ORRERY flagged Drowned Crawlers life cycle before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Drowned Crawlers migration. ORRERY flagged the pale migration before anyone else noticed it. The notes on current-walking run to several pages.

[[dundarushs-cradle|Dundarush's Cradle]] tells the other side of the story. Compare the account in [[sleeping-leviathans|Sleeping Leviathans]]. This connects to [[eliithdar-station|Eliithdar Station]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[operation-ironveil-iv|Operation Ironveil IV]]. Crew who worked on [[paan-drifters|Paan Drifters]] disagreed.
