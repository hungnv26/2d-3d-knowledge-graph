# Drowned Choir


The Drowned Choir were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The room held its breath for eleven minutes. Nobody spoke first, by design, and consent beacons was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Drowned Choir song run to several pages. Drowned Choir life cycle came up in every debrief afterwards. Drowned Choir symbiosis came up in every debrief afterwards. The silent greeting is still an open question in the archive.
