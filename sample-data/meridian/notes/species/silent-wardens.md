# Silent Wardens


Silent Wardens are impossible to watch and harder to classify. The room held its breath for eleven minutes. The protocol said wait, and they waited, and shared-zero mathematics was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Silent Wardens courtship lights run to several pages. Silent Wardens life cycle came up in every debrief afterwards. Silent Wardens song is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what Silent Wardens symbiosis really meant. The survey team spent a week on intent signalling alone.
