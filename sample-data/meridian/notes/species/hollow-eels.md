# Hollow Eels


The archive keeps more questions than answers about the Hollow Eels. The serum held, barely. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and phantom symptoms was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Hollow Eels venom before anyone else noticed it. Hollow Eels courtship lights came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Hollow Eels migration really meant. The survey team spent a week on xeno-allergens alone.
