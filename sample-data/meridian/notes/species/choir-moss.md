# Choir Moss


The Choir Moss were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The water glowed a patient blue. Something below the shelf answered in light, and shoal mind was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Choir Moss life cycle really meant. Choir Moss nesting grounds is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to bioluminescent grammar. ORRERY flagged the pale migration before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on shoal mind alone.
