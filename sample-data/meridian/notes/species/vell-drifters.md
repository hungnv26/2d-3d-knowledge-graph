# Vell Drifters


Vell Drifters are unsettling to watch and harder to classify. The treaty was written in weather, not ink. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and wind-reading was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Vell Drifters migration run to several pages. Vell Drifters life cycle came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on Vell Drifters nesting grounds alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vell Drifters song. Nobody agreed on what Vell migration law really meant. The crew learned about trade in silence the hard way.
