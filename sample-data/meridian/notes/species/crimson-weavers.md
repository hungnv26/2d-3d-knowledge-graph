# Crimson Weavers


The Crimson Weavers were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. Nobody spoke first, by design. A single prime number blinked back, and reciprocal pause was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Crimson Weavers symbiosis the hard way. Crimson Weavers life cycle is still an open question in the archive. Consent beacons came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to cultural firewall.

See also [[commander-luca-ekwueme|Commander Luca Ekwueme]]. The same pattern showed up at [[operation-whisperfield-v|Operation Whisperfield V]]. Crew who worked on [[orrery|ORRERY]] disagreed.

A single prime number blinked back. The room held its breath for eleven minutes.
