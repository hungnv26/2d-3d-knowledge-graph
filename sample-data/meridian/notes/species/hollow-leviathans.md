# Hollow Leviathans


The Hollow Leviathans were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The treaty was written in weather, not ink. The council drifted apart and together like cloud, and the listening embassy was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Hollow Leviathans symbiosis alone. Hollow Leviathans courtship lights came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on Hollow Leviathans song run to several pages. The survey team spent a week on the broken promise alone.

Crew who worked on [[operation-quiet-harbour|Operation Quiet Harbour]] disagreed. See also [[survey-of-melithny-station|Survey of Melithny Station]]. [[vesves-vii-quarantine|Vesves VII Quarantine]] tells the other side of the story. Crew who worked on [[venzorves-vii|Venzorves VII]] disagreed. This connects to [[frozen-grazers|Frozen Grazers]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[orrery|ORRERY]].
