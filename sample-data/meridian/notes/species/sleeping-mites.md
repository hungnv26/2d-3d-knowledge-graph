# Sleeping Mites


The Sleeping Mites were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The tide came in as glass and went out as song. Samples sang faintly in their jars, and bioluminescent grammar was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Sleeping Mites nesting grounds before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged Sleeping Mites song before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged Sleeping Mites courtship lights before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what Sleeping Mites life cycle really meant. Reef memory is still an open question in the archive.

Crew who worked on [[drowned-belt|Drowned Belt]] disagreed. See also [[dareli-choir|Dareli Choir]]. [[orrery|ORRERY]] tells the other side of the story.

The tide came in as glass and went out as song. The water glowed a patient blue.
