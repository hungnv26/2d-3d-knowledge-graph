# Iron Choir


The archive keeps more questions than answers about the Iron Choir. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. The serum held, barely, and bone-density drift was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Iron Choir symbiosis the hard way. The notes on Iron Choir venom run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Iron Choir song. The survey team spent a week on the sickbay ledger alone. Nobody agreed on what long-sleep recovery really meant. Field surgery is still an open question in the archive.
