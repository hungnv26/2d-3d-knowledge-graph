# Broken Leviathans


The Broken Leviathans were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The protocol said wait, and they waited. Nobody spoke first, by design, and intent signalling was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Broken Leviathans life cycle run to several pages. The notes on Broken Leviathans nesting grounds run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what Broken Leviathans courtship lights really meant. The survey team spent a week on Broken Leviathans venom alone. The welcome paradox is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to cultural firewall.
