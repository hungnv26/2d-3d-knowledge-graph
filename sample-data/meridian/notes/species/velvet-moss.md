# Velvet Moss


The Velvet Moss were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. Something below the shelf answered in light. The tide came in as glass and went out as song, and shoal mind was never far from anyone's mind.

Velvet Moss migration is still an open question in the archive. The notes on Velvet Moss nesting grounds run to several pages. The survey team spent a week on spore-song alone.

Compare the account in [[singing-crawlers|Singing Crawlers]]. Crew who worked on [[morka-station-quarantine|Morka Station Quarantine]] disagreed. This connects to [[singing-wardens|Singing Wardens]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[operation-quiet-harbour-vi|Operation Quiet Harbour VI]].
