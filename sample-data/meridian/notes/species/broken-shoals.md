# Broken Shoals


The Broken Shoals were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. Something below the shelf answered in light. Samples sang faintly in their jars, and hydrothermal gardens was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Broken Shoals nesting grounds before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on Broken Shoals venom alone. Reef memory came up in every debrief afterwards.
