# Drowned Leviathans


The Drowned Leviathans were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. Something below the shelf answered in light. Samples sang faintly in their jars, and silica shells was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Drowned Leviathans symbiosis really meant. Drowned Leviathans song is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged Drowned Leviathans life cycle before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on silica shells alone. Lantern chemistry is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on the pale migration alone.
