# Singing Mites


The Singing Mites were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed. A gift was returned, which meant everything, and kinship by weather was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Singing Mites venom before anyone else noticed it. The notes on Singing Mites nesting grounds run to several pages. Singing Mites migration came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about Vell honour debts the hard way. Nobody agreed on what the returned gift really meant. The gift exchange is still an open question in the archive.

See also [[operation-ninth-bell-iii|Operation Ninth Bell III]]. Compare the account in [[glass-tides-of-oru-quarantine|Glass Tides of Oru Quarantine]]. Compare the account in [[dareli-choir|Dareli Choir]].

The treaty was written in weather, not ink. The council drifted apart and together like cloud.
