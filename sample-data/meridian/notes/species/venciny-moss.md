# Venciny Moss


The Venciny Moss were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The fever broke on the ninth day. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and bone-density drift was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Venciny Moss symbiosis run to several pages. Venciny Moss life cycle is still an open question in the archive. The crew learned about the blue fever the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to contagion modelling. Most of the arguments aboard came back to long-sleep recovery.
