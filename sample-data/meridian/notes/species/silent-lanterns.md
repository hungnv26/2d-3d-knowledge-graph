# Silent Lanterns


The Silent Lanterns were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. The tide came in as glass and went out as song. The water glowed a patient blue, and spore-song was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Silent Lanterns migration before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Silent Lanterns symbiosis. Nobody agreed on what the drowning light really meant. The survey team spent a week on brine cathedrals alone.

Crew who worked on [[the-velvet-belt-of-harzormor|The Velvet Belt of Harzormor]] disagreed. This connects to [[graviton-beacon|Graviton beacon]] in ways that are still being argued over. This connects to [[ensign-hana-castellanos|Ensign Hana Castellanos]] in ways that are still being argued over.

Something below the shelf answered in light. The tide came in as glass and went out as song.
