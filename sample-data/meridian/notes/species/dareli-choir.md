# Dareli Choir


The Dareli Choir were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. A gift was returned, which meant everything. The council drifted apart and together like cloud, and the returned gift was never far from anyone's mind.

Dareli Choir migration is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on Dareli Choir life cycle alone. The survey team spent a week on Dareli Choir symbiosis alone. Nobody agreed on what Dareli Choir song really meant. The survey team spent a week on the broken promise alone. Wind-reading came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the returned gift.
