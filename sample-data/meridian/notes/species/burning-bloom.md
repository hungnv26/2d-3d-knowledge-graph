# Burning Bloom


The archive keeps more questions than answers about the Burning Bloom. The treaty was written in weather, not ink. The council drifted apart and together like cloud, and the returned gift was never far from anyone's mind.

Burning Bloom venom is still an open question in the archive. The crew learned about Burning Bloom migration the hard way. The survey team spent a week on Burning Bloom symbiosis alone. Burning Bloom nesting grounds came up in every debrief afterwards. Trade in silence is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to wind-reading.
