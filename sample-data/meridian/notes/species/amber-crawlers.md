# Amber Crawlers


The Amber Crawlers were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict. The fever broke on the ninth day, and radiation burn protocol was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Amber Crawlers venom. ORRERY flagged Amber Crawlers courtship lights before anyone else noticed it. Amber Crawlers symbiosis came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to contagion modelling. ORRERY flagged spore quarantine before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what the blue fever really meant.

Crew who worked on [[operation-ninth-bell-ix|Operation Ninth Bell IX]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[watch-log-2192-210|Watch Log 2192.210]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[operation-ironveil-iii|Operation Ironveil III]]. The same pattern showed up at [[hollow-eels|Hollow Eels]].

Further reading: *Xeno-Allergen Screening* (Adeyemi, 2192).

The serum held, barely. The fever broke on the ninth day.
