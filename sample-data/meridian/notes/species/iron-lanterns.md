# Iron Lanterns


The Iron Lanterns were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. Every leaf was counted twice. Water was the only currency that mattered, and seed vault was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Iron Lanterns life cycle run to several pages. Iron Lanterns migration is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Iron Lanterns venom. Nobody agreed on what Iron Lanterns nesting grounds really meant. Light starvation came up in every debrief afterwards. Algae ration is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on oxygen garden alone.

The harvest came in thin but alive. The green deck smelled of rain that never fell.
