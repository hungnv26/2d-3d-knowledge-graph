# Listening Stones


The Listening Stones were first recorded by a survey drone, then by very nervous people. An hour was missing from every clock aboard. No signal came back, not even static, and unspoken orbit was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Listening Stones life cycle really meant. The crew learned about Listening Stones migration the hard way. The crew learned about the silence the hard way. Nobody agreed on what signal absence really meant.

See also [[null-choir|Null Choir]]. See also [[watch-log-2192-001-the-missing-hour|Watch Log 2192.001 — The Missing Hour]]. See also [[operation-whisperfield-recovery|Operation Whisperfield Recovery]].

The crew spoke in whispers without deciding to. The instruments reported nothing, perfectly.
