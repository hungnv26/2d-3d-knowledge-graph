# Ember Eels


The archive keeps more questions than answers about the Ember Eels. The fever broke on the ninth day. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict, and the Halvorsen serum was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Ember Eels venom really meant. The crew learned about Ember Eels nesting grounds the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ember Eels symbiosis. The survey team spent a week on bone-density drift alone. Field surgery came up in every debrief afterwards. Long-sleep recovery is still an open question in the archive.
