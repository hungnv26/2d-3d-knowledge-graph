# Singing Deeps


Singing Deeps sits at the edge of the Outer Reach, and the first survey team called it beautiful. The tide came in as glass and went out as song. Samples sang faintly in their jars, and bioluminescent grammar was never far from anyone's mind.

Singing Deeps storm cycle came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what Singing Deeps orbital resonance really meant. The Singing Deeps rift came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to hydrothermal gardens. Most of the arguments aboard came back to pressure bloom.

The same pattern showed up at [[xanlimvens-veil|Xanlimven's Veil]]. Compare the account in [[the-drowned-shelf-of-tethzorven|The Drowned Shelf of Tethzorven]]. See also [[thalanra-rays|Thalanra Rays]]. [[sleeping-drifters|Sleeping Drifters]] tells the other side of the story. This connects to [[survey-of-vesven-iii|Survey of Vesven III]] in ways that are still being argued over.
