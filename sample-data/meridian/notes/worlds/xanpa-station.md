# Xanpa Station


Nobody forgets their first approach to Xanpa Station. Space folded like wet paper. Every jump left a faint scar in the charts, and the quiet jump was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about the Xanpa Station rift the hard way. The survey team spent a week on Xanpa Station landing site alone. ORRERY flagged Xanpa Station magnetosphere before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged exotic matter ballast before anyone else noticed it. Harmonic lock is still an open question in the archive.

The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance. The resonance needle climbed past amber.
