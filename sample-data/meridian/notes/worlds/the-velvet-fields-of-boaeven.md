# The Velvet Fields of Boaeven


The Velvet Fields of Boaeven sits at the edge of the Outer Reach, and the first survey team called it lonely. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four. The survey drone came back with more questions, and gravity shoals was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Velvet Fields of Boaeven rift. The notes on Velvet Fields of Boaeven ice shelf run to several pages. Parallax ladders is still an open question in the archive. The dark meridian came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged beacon chains before anyone else noticed it.

This connects to [[ensign-tomas-ekwueme|Ensign Tomas Ekwueme]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[venraov-wardens|Venraov Wardens]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[vestethan-iv|Vestethan IV]] disagreed. Compare the account in [[lattice-crystal|Lattice crystal]]. [[ramels-needle-quarantine|Ramel's Needle Quarantine]] tells the other side of the story. Compare the account in [[operation-lanternfall-iv|Operation Lanternfall IV]].
