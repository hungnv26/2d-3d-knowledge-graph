# Cian's Orchard


Nobody forgets their first approach to Cian's Orchard. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and star-fall was never far from anyone's mind.

Cian's Orchard ice shelf came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on Cian's Orchard landing site run to several pages. The crew learned about orbital census the hard way.

Crew who worked on [[survey-of-anushkir-station|Survey of Anushkir Station]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[xananeli-station-quarantine|Xananeli Station Quarantine]]. Crew who worked on [[the-drowned-spires-of-ululteth|The Drowned Spires of Ululteth]] disagreed. [[survey-of-nypaors-mirror|Survey of Nypaor's Mirror]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[silent-loom|Silent loom]]. [[ramels-needle|Ramel's Needle]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[star-drive|Star drive]].
