# Selharny Station


Charts list Selharny Station as a ruin world at the edge of the Outer Reach. The resonance needle climbed past amber. Space folded like wet paper, and spacetime lensing was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged the Selharny Station beacon before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about Selharny Station landing site the hard way. Nobody agreed on what the Selharny Station rift really meant. The crew learned about drive bloom the hard way.

Every jump left a faint scar in the charts. The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance.
