# Limdunxan Station


Nobody forgets their first approach to Limdunxan Station. Space folded like wet paper. The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance, and the quiet jump was never far from anyone's mind.

The Limdunxan Station rift is still an open question in the archive. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Limdunxan Station storm cycle.
