# Glass Tides


Nobody forgets their first approach to Glass Tides. Nobody spoke first, by design. The room held its breath for eleven minutes, and intent signalling was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about the Glass Tides rift the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Glass Tides tidal lock. The notes on reciprocal pause run to several pages.

The same pattern showed up at [[iron-shelf|Iron Shelf]]. [[rescue-at-silent-vaults-of-cimel|Rescue at Silent Vaults of Cimel]] tells the other side of the story. This connects to [[tethtethlim-ix-quarantine|Tethtethlim IX Quarantine]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[captain-kofi-solberg|Captain Kofi Solberg]] disagreed.
