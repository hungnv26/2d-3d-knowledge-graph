# Stillwater Ring


Nobody forgets their first approach to Stillwater Ring. An hour was missing from every clock aboard. No signal came back, not even static, and dead-air geometry was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Stillwater Ring orbital resonance. Stillwater Ring ice shelf came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the silence.
