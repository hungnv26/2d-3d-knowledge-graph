# Vesves VII


Vesves VII sits along the Vell Drift, and the first survey team called it beautiful. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four. The map was redrawn for the ninth time, and beacon chains was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Vesves VII ice shelf the hard way. ORRERY flagged Vesves VII tidal lock before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Vesves VII rift. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vesves VII landing site. Gravity shoals came up in every debrief afterwards.

See also [[ovteth-iii|Ovteth III]]. The same pattern showed up at [[vesven-iii|Vesven III]]. Compare the account in [[cians-orchard|Cian's Orchard]]. Crew who worked on [[lattice-lance|Lattice lance]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[star-drive|Star drive]] disagreed.

The charts ended and the dark began. The map was redrawn for the ninth time.
