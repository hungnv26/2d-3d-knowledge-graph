# Singing Wastes


Charts list Singing Wastes as a tidal world inside the Lantern Belt. Space folded like wet paper. Every jump left a faint scar in the charts, and the quiet jump was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Singing Wastes landing site alone. Singing Wastes magnetosphere came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Singing Wastes rift. ORRERY flagged exotic matter ballast before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on fold harmonics alone.

Crew who worked on [[ensign-kenji-moreau|Ensign Kenji Moreau]] disagreed. [[orzoran-station|Orzoran Station]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[watch-log-2187-048|Watch Log 2187.048]]. See also [[hull-abacus|Hull abacus]]. This connects to [[operation-seedvault|Operation Seedvault]] in ways that are still being argued over. The same pattern showed up at [[orrery|ORRERY]].

Further reading: *On Tachyon Shear in Crewed Jumps* (Moreau, 2186).
