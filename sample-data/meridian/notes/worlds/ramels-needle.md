# Ramel's Needle


Ramel's Needle sits deep in the Oru system, and the first survey team called it lonely. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The charts ended and the dark began, and dust rivers was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Ramel's Needle magnetosphere really meant. Nobody agreed on what the Ramel's Needle beacon really meant. ORRERY flagged Ramel's Needle orbital resonance before anyone else noticed it. Ramel's Needle landing site came up in every debrief afterwards. Beacon chains came up in every debrief afterwards.

Crew who worked on [[the-drowned-spires-of-ululteth|The Drowned Spires of Ululteth]] disagreed. This connects to [[zoranuls-harbour|Zoranul's Harbour]] in ways that are still being argued over.

The survey drone came back with more questions. The charts ended and the dark began.
