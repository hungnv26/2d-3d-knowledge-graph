# Pale Gardens


Pale Gardens sits along the Vell Drift, and the first survey team called it lonely. The council drifted apart and together like cloud. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and the listening embassy was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Pale Gardens storm cycle before anyone else noticed it. The Pale Gardens rift is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged Pale Gardens landing site before anyone else noticed it. The notes on Vell migration law run to several pages. The survey team spent a week on the Vell alone. The slow treaty came up in every debrief afterwards.
