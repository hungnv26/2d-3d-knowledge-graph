# Stillwater Minor


Nobody forgets their first approach to Stillwater Minor. The instruments reported nothing, perfectly. The crew spoke in whispers without deciding to, and signal absence was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Stillwater Minor orbital resonance the hard way. Stillwater Minor magnetosphere came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what signal absence really meant. The notes on the quiet wound run to several pages.

Compare the account in [[stillwater-deep|Stillwater Deep]]. Compare the account in [[null-choir|Null Choir]]. Crew who worked on [[stillwater-verge|Stillwater Verge]] disagreed.

Further reading: *On the Absence of Signal* (Quill, 2181).
