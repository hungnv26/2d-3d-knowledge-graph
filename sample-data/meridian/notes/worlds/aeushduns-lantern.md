# Aeushdun's Lantern


Nobody forgets their first approach to Aeushdun's Lantern. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and the Outer Reach was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged the Aeushdun's Lantern rift before anyone else noticed it. Aeushdun's Lantern tidal lock came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what the Aeushdun's Lantern beacon really meant. The notes on beacon chains run to several pages.

The same pattern showed up at [[iron-kelp|Iron Kelp]]. See also [[rescue-at-silent-vaults-of-rathalqu|Rescue at Silent Vaults of Rathalqu]]. This connects to [[the-velvet-fields-of-boaeven|The Velvet Fields of Boaeven]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[keplers-lantern|Kepler's Lantern]]. This connects to [[zoranuls-harbour|Zoranul's Harbour]] in ways that are still being argued over. See also [[cikirush-xi-quarantine|Cikirush XI Quarantine]].

The charts ended and the dark began. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four.
