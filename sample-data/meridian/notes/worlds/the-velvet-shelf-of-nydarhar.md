# The Velvet Shelf of Nydarhar


Nobody forgets their first approach to the Velvet Shelf of Nydarhar. The survey drone came back with more questions. The map was redrawn for the ninth time, and waypoint decay was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Velvet Shelf of Nydarhar orbital resonance. Nobody agreed on what Velvet Shelf of Nydarhar ice shelf really meant. Lensed horizons is still an open question in the archive. The notes on gravity shoals run to several pages. Dust rivers came up in every debrief afterwards.

Crew who worked on [[operation-ironveil-iii|Operation Ironveil III]] disagreed. Compare the account in [[the-velvet-fields-of-boaeven|The Velvet Fields of Boaeven]]. This connects to [[ushnyci-leviathans|Ushnyci Leviathans]] in ways that are still being argued over. [[nypaors-mirror|Nypaor's Mirror]] tells the other side of the story.
