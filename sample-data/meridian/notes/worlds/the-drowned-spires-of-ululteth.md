# The Drowned Spires of Ululteth


The Drowned Spires of Ululteth sits deep in the Oru system, and the first survey team called it patient. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four. The map was redrawn for the ninth time, and the long survey was never far from anyone's mind.

Drowned Spires of Ululteth magnetosphere came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Drowned Spires of Ululteth beacon. ORRERY flagged the Drowned Spires of Ululteth rift before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to lensed horizons. The survey team spent a week on star-fall alone.
