# The Iron Fields of Elithal


Charts list the Iron Fields of Elithal as a storm world along the Vell Drift. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. Every chamber faced the same distant point, and the sealed vault was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Iron Fields of Elithal tidal lock. Iron Fields of Elithal orbital resonance is still an open question in the archive. The Iron Fields of Elithal beacon came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged spiral glyphs before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged star-map murals before anyone else noticed it.
