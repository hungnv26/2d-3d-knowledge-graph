# Darelior's Bell


Darelior's Bell sits at the edge of the Outer Reach, and the first survey team called it patient. The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance. The resonance needle climbed past amber, and drive bloom was never far from anyone's mind.

Darelior's Bell storm cycle is still an open question in the archive. Darelior's Bell magnetosphere is still an open question in the archive.
