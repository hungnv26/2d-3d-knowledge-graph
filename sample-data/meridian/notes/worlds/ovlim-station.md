# Ovlim Station


Nobody forgets their first approach to Ovlim Station. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make. Every chamber faced the same distant point, and ossuary gardens was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Ovlim Station tidal lock run to several pages. Ovlim Station ice shelf came up in every debrief afterwards. The Ovlim Station beacon came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Ovlim Station magnetosphere before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about spiral glyphs the hard way. ORRERY flagged the absent builders before anyone else noticed it.

Crew who worked on [[the-silent-vaults-of-rathalqu|The Silent Vaults of Rathalqu]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[iron-fields-of-elithal-quarantine|Iron Fields of Elithal Quarantine]]. This connects to [[survey-of-iron-steppes-of-bothalris|Survey of Iron Steppes of Bothalris]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[orrery|ORRERY]].

The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose.
