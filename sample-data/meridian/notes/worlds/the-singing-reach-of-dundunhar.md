# The Singing Reach of Dundunhar


Charts list the Singing Reach of Dundunhar as a frozen world deep in the Oru system. Every jump left a faint scar in the charts. The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance, and tachyon shear was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Singing Reach of Dundunhar orbital resonance the hard way. Nobody agreed on what the Singing Reach of Dundunhar beacon really meant. Nobody agreed on what null corridor really meant. The survey team spent a week on exotic matter ballast alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to harmonic lock.
