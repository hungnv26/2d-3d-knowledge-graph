# Stillwater Prime


Stillwater Prime sits deep in the Oru system, and the first survey team called it impossible. The crew spoke in whispers without deciding to. The instruments reported nothing, perfectly, and signal absence was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Stillwater Prime beacon. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Stillwater Prime storm cycle. The notes on Stillwater Prime magnetosphere run to several pages. The notes on the listening stones run to several pages. ORRERY flagged the quiet wound before anyone else noticed it. The crew learned about the missing hour the hard way.

The same pattern showed up at [[stillwater-minor|Stillwater Minor]]. Crew who worked on [[stillwater-ring|Stillwater Ring]] disagreed. Crew who worked on [[listening-stones|Listening Stones]] disagreed.
