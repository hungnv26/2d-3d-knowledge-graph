# The Iron Steppes of Bothalris


Charts list the Iron Steppes of Bothalris as a tidal world inside the Lantern Belt. Every chamber faced the same distant point. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them, and spiral glyphs was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what the Iron Steppes of Bothalris beacon really meant. The notes on the Iron Steppes of Bothalris rift run to several pages. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Precursors. Nobody agreed on what harmonic masonry really meant. Keystone engines is still an open question in the archive.

[[operation-low-tide-iv|Operation Low Tide IV]] tells the other side of the story. Compare the account in [[ciithul-ix-quarantine|Ciithul IX Quarantine]]. This connects to [[captains-log-2193-088-salt-blight|Captain's Log 2193.088 — Salt Blight]] in ways that are still being argued over. [[orrery|ORRERY]] tells the other side of the story.
