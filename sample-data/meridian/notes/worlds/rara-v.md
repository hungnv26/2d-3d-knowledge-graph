# Rara V


Charts list Rara V as a ocean world inside the Lantern Belt. The charts ended and the dark began. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and uncharted silence was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on the Rara V beacon alone. Rara V landing site came up in every debrief afterwards. Rara V storm cycle came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on Rara V magnetosphere run to several pages. The cartographer’s error is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged the long survey before anyone else noticed it. The notes on gravity shoals run to several pages.

The survey drone came back with more questions. The charts ended and the dark began.
