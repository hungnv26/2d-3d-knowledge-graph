# Ciithul IX


Nobody forgets their first approach to Ciithul IX. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make, and harmonic masonry was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Ciithul IX magnetosphere really meant. Nobody agreed on what the Ciithul IX rift really meant. Ciithul IX orbital resonance is still an open question in the archive. Star-map murals came up in every debrief afterwards. The lantern script came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about the third inscription the hard way.
