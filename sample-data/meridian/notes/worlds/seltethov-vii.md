# Seltethov VII


Charts list Seltethov VII as a garden world just off the Meridian Line. The charts ended and the dark began. The map was redrawn for the ninth time, and the edge of the map was never far from anyone's mind.

Seltethov VII ice shelf is still an open question in the archive. The crew learned about Seltethov VII orbital resonance the hard way. The survey team spent a week on uncharted silence alone. The crew learned about the Outer Reach the hard way.

The same pattern showed up at [[memory-scrubber|Memory scrubber]]. This connects to [[ensign-tomas-ekwueme|Ensign Tomas Ekwueme]] in ways that are still being argued over.
