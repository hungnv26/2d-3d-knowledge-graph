# Glass Shallows


Nobody forgets their first approach to Glass Shallows. The fold drive hummed at the edge of tolerance. Every jump left a faint scar in the charts, and phase anchoring was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Glass Shallows landing site really meant. The notes on Glass Shallows magnetosphere run to several pages. The Glass Shallows beacon came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on Glass Shallows storm cycle alone. ORRERY flagged exotic matter ballast before anyone else noticed it. The notes on fold scarring run to several pages.
