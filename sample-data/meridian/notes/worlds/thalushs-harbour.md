# Thalush's Harbour


Charts list Thalush's Harbour as a tidal world just off the Meridian Line. The protocol said wait, and they waited. A single prime number blinked back, and the silent greeting was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what the Thalush's Harbour rift really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Thalush's Harbour tidal lock. ORRERY flagged intent signalling before anyone else noticed it.

See also [[rescue-at-kaqus-mirror|Rescue at Kaqu's Mirror]]. Compare the account in [[ormors-bell|Ormor's Bell]].

Nobody spoke first, by design. A single prime number blinked back.
