# The Amber Steppes of Venzorka


Charts list the Amber Steppes of Venzorka as a ocean world just off the Meridian Line. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four. The survey drone came back with more questions, and gravity shoals was never far from anyone's mind.

The Amber Steppes of Venzorka beacon is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on Amber Steppes of Venzorka orbital resonance alone. The notes on the long survey run to several pages.

Crew who worked on [[lattice-harp|Lattice harp]] disagreed. Compare the account in [[cikirush-xi-quarantine|Cikirush XI Quarantine]]. Compare the account in [[uldarmel-vi|Uldarmel VI]]. The same pattern showed up at [[lattice-crystal|Lattice crystal]].

The survey drone came back with more questions. The charts ended and the dark began.
