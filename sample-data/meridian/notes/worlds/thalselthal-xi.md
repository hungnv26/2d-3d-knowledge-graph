# Thalselthal XI


Thalselthal XI sits deep in the Oru system, and the first survey team called it beautiful. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them, and resonant doors was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Thalselthal XI landing site. Thalselthal XI storm cycle came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on Thalselthal XI magnetosphere run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what star-map murals really meant.
