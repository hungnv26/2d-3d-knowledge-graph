# Venthal's Orchard


Venthal's Orchard sits deep in the Oru system, and the first survey team called it beautiful. The charts ended and the dark began. Three beacons flickered where there should have been four, and the Outer Reach was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Venthal's Orchard tidal lock before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what Venthal's Orchard storm cycle really meant. The notes on Venthal's Orchard magnetosphere run to several pages. ORRERY flagged Venthal's Orchard landing site before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the edge of the map.
