# Ululdar's Hearth


Ululdar's Hearth sits inside the Lantern Belt, and the first survey team called it impossible. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them, and harmonic masonry was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Ululdar's Hearth storm cycle the hard way. ORRERY flagged the Ululdar's Hearth rift before anyone else noticed it. The sealed vault came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what star-map murals really meant. Keystone engines is still an open question in the archive.

This connects to [[the-iron-steppes-of-bothalris|The Iron Steppes of Bothalris]] in ways that are still being argued over. The same pattern showed up at [[hollow-spires|Hollow Spires]]. Compare the account in [[operation-lanternfall-v|Operation Lanternfall V]].

The builders had left, carefully, on purpose. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them.
