# Limthalven Station


Charts list Limthalven Station as a desert world deep in the Oru system. The charts ended and the dark began. The survey drone came back with more questions, and the Outer Reach was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Limthalven Station tidal lock alone. ORRERY flagged the Limthalven Station rift before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the cartographer’s error. Nobody agreed on what the edge of the map really meant.
