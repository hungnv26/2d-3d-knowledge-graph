# Vestethan IV


Nobody forgets their first approach to Vestethan IV. The survey drone came back with more questions. The charts ended and the dark began, and star-fall was never far from anyone's mind.

The Vestethan IV rift came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged Vestethan IV landing site before anyone else noticed it. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vestethan IV ice shelf. Vestethan IV orbital resonance is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what uncharted silence really meant.

Compare the account in [[the-hungry-steppes-of-ulmelor|The Hungry Steppes of Ulmelor]]. Compare the account in [[ramels-needle|Ramel's Needle]]. [[operation-glasswing-v|Operation Glasswing V]] tells the other side of the story.

The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The charts ended and the dark began.
