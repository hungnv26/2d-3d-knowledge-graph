# The Hollow Fields of Quvesthal


The Hollow Fields of Quvesthal sits along the Vell Drift, and the first survey team called it lonely. A single prime number blinked back. Nobody spoke first, by design, and the non-interference line was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Hollow Fields of Quvesthal storm cycle before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on the Hollow Fields of Quvesthal beacon alone. Nobody agreed on what Hollow Fields of Quvesthal tidal lock really meant. Contact quarantine came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to shared-zero mathematics.
