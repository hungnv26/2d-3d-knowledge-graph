# Hollow Steppes


Hollow Steppes sits along the Vell Drift, and the first survey team called it patient. The resonance needle climbed past amber. Every jump left a faint scar in the charts, and fold scarring was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Hollow Steppes orbital resonance before anyone else noticed it. The notes on Hollow Steppes storm cycle run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what resonance drift really meant.

Compare the account in [[nyriss-cradle|Nyris's Cradle]]. Crew who worked on [[spore-anchor|Spore anchor]] disagreed. This connects to [[navigator-bao-achterberg|Navigator Bao Achterberg]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[specialist-luca-achterberg|Specialist Luca Achterberg]].

Space folded like wet paper. The resonance needle climbed past amber.
