# Xananeli Station


Nobody forgets their first approach to Xananeli Station. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose, and the lantern script was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged the Xananeli Station rift before anyone else noticed it. Xananeli Station storm cycle came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on the absent builders alone. Buried observatories came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on the patient architecture alone.
