# Hungry Deeps


Hungry Deeps sits just off the Meridian Line, and the first survey team called it generous. The room held its breath for eleven minutes. The protocol said wait, and they waited, and translation lattice was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Hungry Deeps tidal lock run to several pages. Hungry Deeps landing site came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about Hungry Deeps ice shelf the hard way.
