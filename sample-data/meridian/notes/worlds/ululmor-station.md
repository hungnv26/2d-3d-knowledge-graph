# Ululmor Station


Nobody forgets their first approach to Ululmor Station. A gift was returned, which meant everything. The Vell spoke only when the wind agreed, and kinship by weather was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Ululmor Station ice shelf alone. Ululmor Station storm cycle is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what the Ululmor Station rift really meant. The broken promise came up in every debrief afterwards.

Compare the account in [[singing-mites|Singing Mites]]. Crew who worked on [[hollow-reach|Hollow Reach]] disagreed. The same pattern showed up at [[iron-kelp|Iron Kelp]].

Further reading: *Notes on Drift-Speech Phonology* (Vantablack, 2192).
