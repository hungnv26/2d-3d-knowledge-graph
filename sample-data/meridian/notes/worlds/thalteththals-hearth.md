# Thalteththal's Hearth


Nobody forgets their first approach to Thalteththal's Hearth. The charts ended and the dark began. The map was redrawn for the ninth time, and the long survey was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Thalteththal's Hearth tidal lock really meant. The notes on Thalteththal's Hearth ice shelf run to several pages. The crew learned about Thalteththal's Hearth storm cycle the hard way. Nobody agreed on what uncharted silence really meant.
