# Amber Reach


Nobody forgets their first approach to Amber Reach. Every chamber faced the same distant point. The doorway opened at a hum only the ship could make, and ossuary gardens was never far from anyone's mind.

Amber Reach tidal lock came up in every debrief afterwards. Amber Reach magnetosphere is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged buried observatories before anyone else noticed it.

Compare the account in [[vestethan-iv-quarantine|Vestethan IV Quarantine]]. This connects to [[thalselthal-xi|Thalselthal XI]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[ion-beacon|Ion beacon]] disagreed.

Further reading: *On The Drowned Library Hypothesis* (Nakamura, 2184).
