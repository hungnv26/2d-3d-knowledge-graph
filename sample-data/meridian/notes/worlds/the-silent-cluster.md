# The Silent Cluster


Nobody forgets their first approach to the Silent Cluster. The crew spoke in whispers without deciding to. The instruments reported nothing, perfectly, and the silence was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Silent Cluster orbital resonance before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged Silent Cluster ice shelf before anyone else noticed it. Silent Cluster tidal lock came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about the stillwater effect the hard way. Unspoken orbit came up in every debrief afterwards.

Crew who worked on [[stillwater-deep|Stillwater Deep]] disagreed. This connects to [[listening-stones|Listening Stones]] in ways that are still being argued over. This connects to [[stillwater-ring|Stillwater Ring]] in ways that are still being argued over.

No signal came back, not even static. The instruments reported nothing, perfectly.
