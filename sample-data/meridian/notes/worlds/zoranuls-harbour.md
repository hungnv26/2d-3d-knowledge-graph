# Zoranul's Harbour


Nobody forgets their first approach to Zoranul's Harbour. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The survey drone came back with more questions, and waypoint decay was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Zoranul's Harbour magnetosphere the hard way. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Zoranul's Harbour rift. Zoranul's Harbour tidal lock came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the dark meridian.

The same pattern showed up at [[neural-loom|Neural loom]]. Compare the account in [[the-hungry-steppes-of-ulmelor|The Hungry Steppes of Ulmelor]]. The same pattern showed up at [[nypaors-mirror|Nypaor's Mirror]]. Crew who worked on [[orrery|ORRERY]] disagreed.

The charts ended and the dark began. The survey drone came back with more questions.
