# The Glass Tides of Oru


Nobody forgets their first approach to the Glass Tides of Oru. Something below the shelf answered in light. The tide came in as glass and went out as song, and lantern chemistry was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Glass Tides of Oru ice shelf really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Glass Tides of Oru landing site. ORRERY flagged the drowning light before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what bioluminescent grammar really meant.

[[cians-orchard-quarantine|Cian's Orchard Quarantine]] tells the other side of the story. The same pattern showed up at [[velvet-lanterns|Velvet Lanterns]]. Compare the account in [[sleeping-leviathans|Sleeping Leviathans]]. Crew who worked on [[captain-amara-zhao|Captain Amara Zhao]] disagreed.
