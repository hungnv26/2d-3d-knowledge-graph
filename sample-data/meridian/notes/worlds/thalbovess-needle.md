# Thalboves's Needle


Charts list Thalboves's Needle as a desert world deep in the Oru system. The survey drone came back with more questions. The charts ended and the dark began, and drift charts was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Thalboves's Needle ice shelf really meant. Nobody agreed on what Thalboves's Needle orbital resonance really meant. The survey team spent a week on star-fall alone. ORRERY flagged drift charts before anyone else noticed it. The notes on waypoint decay run to several pages.

Compare the account in [[quantum-scrubber|Quantum scrubber]]. This connects to [[operation-keystone|Operation Keystone]] in ways that are still being argued over. Crew who worked on [[orrery|ORRERY]] disagreed.
