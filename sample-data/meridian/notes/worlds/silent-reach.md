# Silent Reach


Nobody forgets their first approach to Silent Reach. The room held its breath for eleven minutes. Nobody spoke first, by design, and signal humility was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Silent Reach tidal lock alone. ORRERY flagged Silent Reach landing site before anyone else noticed it. The Silent Reach rift came up in every debrief afterwards. The notes on the welcome paradox run to several pages.

See also [[ion-furnace|Ion furnace]]. Crew who worked on [[thalushs-harbour|Thalush's Harbour]] disagreed. This connects to [[orrery|ORRERY]] in ways that are still being argued over.
