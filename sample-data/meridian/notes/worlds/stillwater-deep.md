# Stillwater Deep


Stillwater Deep sits along the Vell Drift, and the first survey team called it unsettling. The crew spoke in whispers without deciding to. An hour was missing from every clock aboard, and the stillwater effect was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Stillwater Deep magnetosphere really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Stillwater Deep storm cycle. Most of the arguments aboard came back to dead-air geometry. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the silence. Nobody agreed on what signal absence really meant.

Crew who worked on [[operation-whisperfield-recovery|Operation Whisperfield Recovery]] disagreed. Compare the account in [[stillwater-ring|Stillwater Ring]]. Crew who worked on [[incident-report-dead-air-2192|Incident Report: Dead Air (2192)]] disagreed.

An hour was missing from every clock aboard. The instruments reported nothing, perfectly.
