# Vesven III


Charts list Vesven III as a storm world inside the Lantern Belt. The map was redrawn for the ninth time. The charts ended and the dark began, and orbital census was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Vesven III magnetosphere. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Vesven III beacon. The crew learned about Vesven III ice shelf the hard way. Vesven III landing site came up in every debrief afterwards. Lensed horizons came up in every debrief afterwards. Nobody agreed on what drift charts really meant. Nobody agreed on what star-fall really meant.
