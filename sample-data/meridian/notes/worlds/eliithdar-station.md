# Eliithdar Station


Charts list Eliithdar Station as a tidal world just off the Meridian Line. The tide came in as glass and went out as song. The water glowed a patient blue, and lantern chemistry was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Eliithdar Station landing site the hard way. ORRERY flagged Eliithdar Station ice shelf before anyone else noticed it. Nobody agreed on what reef memory really meant. Brine cathedrals came up in every debrief afterwards. The pale migration came up in every debrief afterwards.
