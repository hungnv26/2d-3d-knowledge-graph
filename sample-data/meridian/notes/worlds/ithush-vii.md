# Ithush VII


Charts list Ithush VII as a ruin world along the Vell Drift. The builders had left, carefully, on purpose. The glyphs were older than the star that lit them, and the drowned library was never far from anyone's mind.

Ithush VII tidal lock came up in every debrief afterwards. Ithush VII landing site is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on Ithush VII storm cycle alone. Chronolith came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged resonant doors before anyone else noticed it.

Crew who worked on [[operation-low-tide-iv|Operation Low Tide IV]] disagreed. This connects to [[ciithul-ix|Ciithul IX]] in ways that are still being argued over. The same pattern showed up at [[cikirush-xi|Cikirush XI]]. The same pattern showed up at [[the-singing-deeps-of-kirdar|The Singing Deeps of Kirdar]].
