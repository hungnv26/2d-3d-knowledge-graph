# Velvet Steppes


Nobody forgets their first approach to Velvet Steppes. The water glowed a patient blue. The tide came in as glass and went out as song, and the drowning light was never far from anyone's mind.

Most of the arguments aboard came back to Velvet Steppes tidal lock. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the Velvet Steppes beacon. ORRERY flagged hydrothermal gardens before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged bioluminescent grammar before anyone else noticed it. Lantern chemistry is still an open question in the archive.
