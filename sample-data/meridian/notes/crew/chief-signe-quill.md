# Chief Signe Quill


Chief Signe Quill joined the Meridian in the second year and never quite left. Samples sang faintly in their jars. Something below the shelf answered in light, and pressure bloom was never far from anyone's mind.

ORRERY flagged Signe Quill’s commendation before anyone else noticed it. The survey team spent a week on Signe Quill’s command style alone. Bioluminescent grammar is still an open question in the archive.
