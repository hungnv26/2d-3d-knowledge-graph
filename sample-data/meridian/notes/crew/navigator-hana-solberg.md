# Navigator Hana Solberg


The archive remembers Navigator Hana Solberg for more than the service record says. Someone had strung lights along the corridor again. Laughter carried further than it should in the low gravity, and homesick hour was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Hana Solberg’s last posting alone. Hana Solberg’s field journal is still an open question in the archive. The survey team spent a week on the captain’s table alone. First-snow tradition came up in every debrief afterwards. The survey team spent a week on grief rotation alone.

The midwatch supper ran long and nobody minded. Letters from home were read aloud, twice.
