# Navigator Tallulah Yilmaz


The archive remembers Navigator Tallulah Yilmaz for more than the service record says. The midwatch supper ran long and nobody minded. Someone had strung lights along the corridor again, and the chess ladder was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Tallulah Yilmaz’s last posting run to several pages. Nobody agreed on what Tallulah Yilmaz’s research really meant. Nobody agreed on what Tallulah Yilmaz’s commendation really meant. Nobody agreed on what watch rotation really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to the captain’s table.
