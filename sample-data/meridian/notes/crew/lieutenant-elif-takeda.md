# Lieutenant Elif Takeda


Lieutenant Elif Takeda kept the ship honest, according to most of the crew. The fever broke on the ninth day. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone, and the sickbay ledger was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Elif Takeda’s research alone. ORRERY flagged Elif Takeda’s command style before anyone else noticed it. The notes on Elif Takeda’s field journal run to several pages. The sickbay ledger is still an open question in the archive. Nobody agreed on what radiation burn protocol really meant.

Crew who worked on [[survey-of-quventeth-vii|Survey of Quventeth VII]] disagreed. This connects to [[watch-log-2194-211|Watch Log 2194.211]] in ways that are still being argued over. Compare the account in [[dr-elif-solberg|Dr. Elif Solberg]]. See also [[memory-loom|Memory loom]]. [[cryo-crystal|Cryo crystal]] tells the other side of the story.
