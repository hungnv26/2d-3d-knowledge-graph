# Captain Ravi Castellanos


The archive remembers Captain Ravi Castellanos for more than the service record says. Someone had strung lights along the corridor again. The midwatch supper ran long and nobody minded, and watch rotation was never far from anyone's mind.

The survey team spent a week on Ravi Castellanos’s commendation alone. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Ravi Castellanos’s command style. Ravi Castellanos’s field journal came up in every debrief afterwards. The crew learned about Ravi Castellanos’s research the hard way. The notes on deck-four choir run to several pages.
