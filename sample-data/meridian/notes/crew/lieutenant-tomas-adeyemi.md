# Lieutenant Tomas Adeyemi


Lieutenant Tomas Adeyemi kept the ship honest, according to most of the crew. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest. They built what they needed out of what they found, and the junkyard fleet was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Tomas Adeyemi’s field journal the hard way. The notes on Tomas Adeyemi’s commendation run to several pages. The survey team spent a week on the cannibal refit alone.

The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest.
