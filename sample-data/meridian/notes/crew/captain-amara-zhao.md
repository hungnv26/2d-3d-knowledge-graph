# Captain Amara Zhao


The archive remembers Captain Amara Zhao for more than the service record says. Sickbay went quiet in the way that frightened everyone. The serum held, barely, and cryo triage was never far from anyone's mind.

Nobody agreed on what Amara Zhao’s research really meant. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Amara Zhao’s command style. ORRERY flagged long-sleep recovery before anyone else noticed it.

The same pattern showed up at [[watch-log-2192-094|Watch Log 2192.094]]. Crew who worked on [[frozen-kelp|Frozen Kelp]] disagreed. Compare the account in [[incident-report-sensor-ghosts-2194|Incident Report: Sensor Ghosts (2194)]].

Further reading: *On Cryo Triage Outcomes* (Carrow, 2183).

The fever broke on the ninth day. Quarantine doors sealed with a sound like a verdict.
