# Commander Mei Moreau


The archive remembers Commander Mei Moreau for more than the service record says. Someone had strung lights along the corridor again. Letters from home were read aloud, twice, and the midwatch supper was never far from anyone's mind.

Mei Moreau’s research came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to Mei Moreau’s commendation. Nobody agreed on what Mei Moreau’s last posting really meant. The survey team spent a week on Mei Moreau’s command style alone. The chess ladder came up in every debrief afterwards. ORRERY flagged deck-four choir before anyone else noticed it.

[[watch-log-2194-211|Watch Log 2194.211]] tells the other side of the story. See also [[incident-report-archive-flood-2189|Incident Report: Archive Flood (2189)]].

Laughter carried further than it should in the low gravity. The midwatch supper ran long and nobody minded.
