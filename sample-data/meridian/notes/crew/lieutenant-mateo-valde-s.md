# Lieutenant Mateo Valdés


Lieutenant Mateo Valdés joined the Meridian in the second year and never quite left. The derelict gave up its parts without complaint. Three welds and a prayer kept the reactor honest, and structural fatigue was never far from anyone's mind.

The crew learned about Mateo Valdés’s field journal the hard way. Mateo Valdés’s last posting is still an open question in the archive. ORRERY flagged Mateo Valdés’s research before anyone else noticed it. ORRERY flagged stress fractures before anyone else noticed it. The cannibal refit came up in every debrief afterwards.
