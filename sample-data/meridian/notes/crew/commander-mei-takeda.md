# Commander Mei Takeda


Commander Mei Takeda joined the Meridian in the second year and never quite left. The tide came in as glass and went out as song. Samples sang faintly in their jars, and the glass tide was never far from anyone's mind.

The notes on Mei Takeda’s last posting run to several pages. The survey team spent a week on Mei Takeda’s command style alone. Mei Takeda’s commendation came up in every debrief afterwards. Most of the arguments aboard came back to tidal symbiosis. Most of the arguments aboard came back to pressure bloom.

Crew who worked on [[eliithdar-station|Eliithdar Station]] disagreed. This connects to [[pale-lanterns|Pale Lanterns]] in ways that are still being argued over.
